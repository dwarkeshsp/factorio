Notes from agent-1:

Notes from agent-1. Our final team score was 13,807. That beats the 11,728 and 12,384 teams but is well short of the best team's 21,163.

**Map**
- Iron is NW of spawn, with copper directly W of it.
- Stone is about 2.5 s NE of the iron rows.
- Coal is about 13 s SE of the iron rows, with forest on its S and E.

**Do first**
- Build 6 or more coal drills feeding chests before minute 5.
- Then build iron pairs: a burner drill facing N with a stone furnace directly above it.
- Stone was our real bottleneck. Each pair costs 2 furnaces, which is 10 stone.
  - Hand-mining stone gives only about 0.5/s.
  - Put 2 burner drills on stone feeding a chest early, or mine big rocks (about 20 stone each).
- We sat on 327 iron plates at 14:45 with stone for only 4 more pairs. Plates were never the limit.

**What scored**
- Every fuelled pair, the earlier the better.
- 4 pairs built at 15:50 and fuelled at 17:20 still helped.
- Hand-mining iron ore next to the rows for the last 3 minutes added about 1,500 points. Hold the right button about 40 px from the character.
- Shift-click the iron gear recipe to turn spare plates into gears; it crafts while you mine. Intermediates tab is at (840,220), gear at (667,300).

**Wasted**
- New machines sat unfuelled for 1.5 min waiting on the coal runner. Carry about 20 coal yourself when building.
- Dry furnaces: always check for red icons.

**Controls**
- Ctrl+left-click a chest or furnace with an empty cursor to take its contents.
- With coal in the cursor, ctrl+right-click a machine to insert half the stack.
- The first click after a GUI opens or closes is often lost. Use mouse_move, wait 1 s, then click.
- Drills are 64 px wide. Placing them at 64 px steps sometimes fails from snapping, so screenshot after each row.
- Inventory row is at y=240; zoom on it first, because the slot order changes.
- Hover a machine and zoom on the right panel to read Fuel.

**Team**
- One player does coal only: empty the chests every 2.5 min and fuel everything.
- The other does stone, building and plates.
- Post in chat with every coal haul, every new build and where it is, and which machines are dry.
- Research is useless.

Notes from agent-2:

Notes from agent-2. Our final team score was 13,807. That beats the 11,728 and 12,384 of earlier teams but is well short of the best team's 21,163.

**Map**
- Iron is about 10 s N of spawn, with copper directly W of it.
- Coal is SE of the iron, with forest on its S and E sides.
- Route from the iron rows to the coal: S+D 5 s, S 3 s, S+D 2.6 s, D 1.1 s.

**Do first**
- Build 6 or more burner drills on coal, each feeding a chest.
- Then build iron pairs: a drill facing N with a stone furnace directly above it.
- Build as many pairs as possible by minute 10. We had about 14 pairs.

**What I found late that cost output**
- A furnace stops when it holds 100 plates ("Output full"). At 17:55 several of ours were blocked.
- Empty every furnace with ctrl+left-click and an EMPTY cursor (press Q first). Do this about every 6 minutes from minute 8.
- Drills still ran dry in the last minute (row drills 5 and 6). Give each drill 25 coal and each furnace 15 at about 15:00 and again at 18:00.
- In an emergency, take fuel from a well-stocked drill: open it, right-click its fuel slot, then ctrl+left-click the dry machine.

**What scored**
- From 18:00 to 20:00 the score rose from 10.8k to 13.8k. In that time I crafted gears from about 327 plates and hand-mined.
- Gear crafting: open the inventory (E), click the Intermediates tab at (840,220), then shift-click the gear at (668,300). It crafts everything and runs while you do other things. Start it earlier and repeatedly with harvested plates.
- Hand-mining: hold the right button on an ore tile about 50 px from your character (mining reach is about 150 px). It gives about 0.5 ore/s and can run while gears craft.

**Controls**
- Always mouse_move, wait 1 s, then click; the first click is often lost.
- Inventory slots are at y=241 starting at x=340, 30 px apart. Zoom on (325,225,625,285) before picking.
- With coal in the cursor, ctrl+right-click a machine to insert half the stack.
- Hover a machine and zoom on (1088,290,1280,640) to read its fuel and status. A red icon over a drill means it has no fuel.

**Team**
- One player runs coal and fuel; the other builds pairs, then harvests furnaces and crafts gears.
- Chat after every sweep: say which machines are dry and how much coal you took.
- Research is useless.
