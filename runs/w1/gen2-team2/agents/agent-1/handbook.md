MAP: Iron is NW of spawn, and copper adjoins it directly to the W. Coal is far SE, about 15–20 s of walking, inside a dense forest. Approach coal only from its W/NW open sand: from the iron row, go S about 8.5 s, then D about 5.5 s. Going straight SE traps you in trees for minutes; it cost me 90 s at 16:30.

FIRST: Build coal self-sufficiency before the rows.
- Put several coal drills feeding chests (not furnaces) near the coal.
- One player carries coal to the rows every 2–3 min.
- Fuel starvation capped our score.

SCORED: Burner drill facing N with a stone furnace directly above it, on iron and copper. Build as many pairs as possible by minute 10. Also count on hand-mined coal or ore (about 1.2/s while holding the right button 1 tile from the character) and handcrafting plates into gears (iron) and cable (copper).

WASTED:
- I accidentally crafted 6 boilers: after Steam Power research, Production row 1 is the boiler and the drill moves to row 2. Always hover a recipe before clicking.
- A drill feeding into a furnace used as a coal buffer stalls at "Target full" once the fuel slot hits 50. Use a chest instead.
- Walking into forests. If stuck, hold the right button on adjacent trees to clear them; the selection box shows which tree is targeted.
- Research is useless.

CONTROLS:
- Inventory slots are at y=240, x=340/370/400…, and their order changes, so zoom in before picking.
- Pick an item, close with E, click in the world to place, then press Q to clear the cursor. R rotates.
- Ctrl+right-click an entity with coal in the cursor inserts half the stack.
- To open a furnace: click it, then shift-click its fuel slot (667,432) to take the coal.
- Hover a machine to read "Fuel:" on the right panel.
- Escape with no window open shows the Game menu; Resume is at (639,290).
- In the crafting menu, shift-click crafts all.

TEAM: One player handles coal and fuel runs, the other builds and empties the rows. Chat every few minutes with what is dry. Around minute 14, overfuel everything; unburnt coal still counts as score.
