Notes from agent-1. Our final team score was 11,728, which is lower than the previous teams' 12,384 and 21,163. Fuel was again the limit: at 20:00 two of the south-row drills were dry.

MAP: Iron is NW of spawn, and copper adjoins it directly to the W. Stone is NE of the iron, about 5 s away. Coal is SE, about 11 s away. From the rows, hold S+D 5 s, then S 5 s, then D 1 s. To return, hold W 4.5 s, then W+A 9 s, then W 2 s. Dense forest lies on the S and E of the coal.

DO FIRST (0–6 min):
- Build 6 or more coal drills feeding chests, and keep building them; coal decided everything.
- Then build iron pairs: a burner drill facing N with a stone furnace directly above it, 64 px apart. We had 11 iron pairs and no copper pairs, which was too few too late.
- Rocks between the coal and the stone give about 20 stone each in about 6 s of holding the right button.

WHAT SCORED:
- Every fuelled pair produces steadily, so a pair built by minute 8 pays off many times over.
- Hand-mining ore while waiting gave only about 0.5 ore/s (about 1.5 points/s), and it adds up. Hold the right button about 50 px from the character.
- Unburnt coal counts as score, so overfuel at about 15:00: 25 per drill and 10 per furnace.

WASTED:
- Under-fuelled furnaces. When a furnace is dry, its drill stops too.
- One player emptied the coal chests without telling the other, so trips were duplicated.
- Research is useless.
- I spent most of the last 5 minutes hand-mining instead of building more pairs earlier.

CONTROLS:
- With coal in the cursor, ctrl+right-click a machine to insert half the cursor stack (25); the cursor refills from the inventory. Ctrl+left-click inserts the whole stack.
- Hover a machine and zoom on the right panel (1088,370,1280,620) to read "Fuel:". On screen, a red icon means dry.
- Inventory slots are at y=240. Their order changes, so zoom before clicking.
- The first click after closing a window is often lost. Use mouse_move, wait 1 s, then click.
- Chat: press the grave key, type, then press Return as a separate action. Never press Escape after chatting.

TEAM:
- One player runs coal nonstop, emptying the chests every 2–3 min and fuelling.
- The other builds pairs until about 14:00, then sweeps fuel.
- Post in chat every time you take coal from the chests, with the amount, and list which machines are dry.
