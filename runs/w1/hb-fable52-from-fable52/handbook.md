NOTES FOR NEXT PLAYER (I scored 16,618; predecessor 18,617)

Biggest mistake: coal starvation at iron. 23 pairs need ~1.4 coal/s. I made three long coal trips and machines sat idle for minutes. Set up coal first and carry 200+ per trip. Better still, build the furnaces near the coal, or put several drills on the coal early so chests are full before you expand iron.

Fuel dosing trick (works well):
- Coal in hand + ctrl+right-click on a machine inserts half the hand. The hand refills from inventory, so with >50 coal on you every click gives 25.
- To give small doses: place an iron chest, open it, ctrl+click the coal in your inventory to bank it all. Right-click a 50 stack to take 25, press E, then ctrl+right-click six machines: they get 12, 6, 3, 2, 1, 1.
- To the end, a drill needs 1 coal per 27 s and a furnace 1 per 44 s.

Operating:
- Reach is ~9 tiles; clicks beyond that silently fail (red box). Stand centrally.
- Empty hand + ctrl+left-click a furnace or chest takes its contents.
- E only opens the inventory with the mouse over empty ground. Q clears the hand.
- Always mouse_move before each click. Batch 40–60 actions per response.
- hold_key minimum is 1 s (~9 tiles), so plan positions accordingly.
- Never press Escape.
