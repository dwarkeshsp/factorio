NOTES FOR NEXT PLAYER (I scored only 7,833; agent-1 scored 18,617, so follow agent-1's notes first)

Map: iron ore is about 45 tiles W and 40 N of spawn. Copper is just NW of the iron. Coal is about 12 s walk SE of the iron. Stone is about 5 s walk NE of the iron.
- Walk iron→coal: hold s+d 9 s, then s 2 s.
- Walk coal→iron: hold w+a 9 s, then w 2 s.

My biggest failure was fuel starvation.
- At 20:00 almost every drill and furnace in my 8-pair iron row showed a red no-fuel icon.
- The stone drills ran dry by about 11:00.
- I spent minutes dosing coal 1 piece at a time. Bring at least 100 coal per visit, and fuel every drill with 15+ and every furnace with 8+.
- Unburned coal still counts as score, so over-fuel freely.

What earned the most:
1. Drill→furnace pairs on iron (drill directly south of the furnace, default north facing). Build many early.
2. Hand-crafting burner inserters (1 plate + 1 gear; 3 plates of iron and 1 s total each).
   - Shift+left-click a recipe crafts the MAX possible. That queued 116 inserters in one click, so use it.
   - The gears craft first, then the inserters.
   - My score rate went from about 8 to 16 pts/s while crafting.
3. Collecting plates with ctrl+left-click on each furnace (empty hand, within about 6 tiles) and immediately shift-crafting them.

Operating tips:
- E opens the inventory only with the mouse over empty ground; I used (300,600).
- Crafting tabs: Logistics (683,220), Production (762,220), Intermediates (840,220). Burner inserter is at (668,330) in the Logistics tab.
- Fuel through a machine's GUI:
  - Left-click the coal stack in the inventory, then LEFT-click the fuel slot. This inserts the whole hand, which is reliable.
  - Right-clicking the fuel slot inserts only 1 per click and sometimes drops clicks.
  - Slot positions: drill fuel (667,396); furnace fuel (667,435); furnace output (937,390).
- If you are holding plates when you close a GUI, click an empty inventory slot first to put them away.
- Holding right-click to hand-mine ore gave me almost nothing. Don't bother.
- Press Q before moving on, or you may accidentally place a held building.
- Never press Escape.
