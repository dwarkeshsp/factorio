Notes from agent-1 (final team score 14,360 at 20:00)

Map:
- Spawn has a big lake to the east.
- Iron is NW of spawn, 2–3 screens away. Copper is right next to it on the west, and stone is north of both.
- Coal is a small patch S/SE of spawn, with forest on its south and east sides. Walk around through the open sand to the west; walking into the trees gets you stuck.

What to do first:
- Fuel was our bottleneck the whole game. Drills and furnaces sat idle with no coal.
- Early on, build a self-feeding coal setup:
  - Put one burner drill on the coal and a second drill directly north of it, both facing north.
  - The bottom drill feeds the top drill's fuel slot. The top drill outputs into a chest on its top-left tile.
- Fuel the bottom drill once; the top drill then needs nothing.
- Make many of these chains. Late in the game we had about 15 drills on coal, and the score rose fastest then (about 1,100 per minute).

What scored well:
- More burner drills, built as early as possible.
- Hand-mining coal: hold the right mouse button on the ore. It gives about 0.6 per second and is steady score while you wait, so never stand idle.
- One teammate crafted red science for score.

What wasted time:
- Nobody built a lab or queued research. Decide this in the first minute or skip it.
- Fumbling with the GUI and walking back and forth between patches.

Operating the game:
- E (inventory) often doesn't register. Left-click empty ground first, then press E, then check with a screenshot.
- Entity windows often need two clicks or a 2-second wait. Shift-click only works once the window is actually open.
- To place something: click the item in your inventory, press Escape, click the map, then press Q to clear the cursor.
- To refuel: hold coal on the cursor and ctrl+click the drill.
- Hovering over something shows its fuel and contents in the right panel.
- To walk, use hold_key for 0.3–1 seconds (about 9 tiles per second).
- Chat: press `, type the message, then press Enter.

Team split:
- One player on coal chains and refuelling.
- Two on iron/copper drills and furnaces.
- One crafting: drills first, then lab and science.
- Post short chat messages that say where you put shared items, for example "50 stone in E iron chest at coal".
