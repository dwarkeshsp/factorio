**Notes from team 1 (final score 14,360 at 20:00)**

**Map.** Iron and copper are adjacent, NW of spawn (copper on the W side, iron on the E). Stone is N of spawn. Coal is far SE, about 12 s of diagonal walking from the iron, and surrounded by forest. A big lake lies NE of the coal.

**What scored.** Score grew mainly from the number of burner drills kept running.
- Drills dropping ore into stone furnaces (ore 3 pts, plate 6) were the biggest earner.
- Drills on coal feeding chests were the next biggest.
- From 13:00 to 20:00 the score went from about 7k to 14k as drill count grew. Build drills and furnaces as early and as continuously as possible.
- Hand mining is only about 1.5 pts/s. Do it only while waiting.

**What wasted time.**
- Fuel starvation was the biggest loss. Many drills sat with red no-fuel icons.
- One player should be a dedicated fueler who walks a loop, giving each drill and furnace about 10 coal.
- Coal drills pointed into each other (drill→drill chains) help.
- Power and labs never got built, and research was irrelevant to score in 20 minutes. Skip it.
- Walking between coal and iron is about 12 s each way. Carry big coal loads rather than many small trips.

**Controls.**
- E opens inventory. The production tab is in the crafting panel.
- After Escape, the first click on an entity selects it and the second opens its GUI. Wait about 1 s between them.
- Shift-click a stack in the GUI to auto-insert it. Right-click takes half a stack, then click the fuel slot.
- A red selection box means the target is out of reach (about 100 px). Walk closer.
- R rotates before placing; check the yellow arrow points into the furnace. Your own character can block placement.
- Clicks sometimes miss, so verify with a screenshot.
- Hand-mine with mouse_hold right for 30–60 s on a tile next to you, and check that its box is yellow.
- Watch that clicks don't open a nearby drill by accident.

**Team split.**
- 2 players: mine stone, craft furnaces and drills, and place them on iron and copper.
- 1 player: builds coal drills into chests.
- 1 player: fuels everything continuously.
- Announce in chat (grave key, type, Return) where chests and new unfuelled drills are. Say when you empty a chest so others don't walk to it.
- In the last minute, craft gears or drills from loose plates.
