Final team score: 14,360. No power was ever built; research stopped after Automation science.

Map:
- Iron patch is NW of start, copper W/NW of iron, a lake far east.
- Coal patch is about 15 tiles SE of start.

What to do first:
- Hand-mine stone and iron and get stone furnaces smelting at once.
- Craft burner mining drills as early as possible. Each drill needs a chest on the tile at its output arrow; check the arrow before placing the chest.
- Drills keep producing with no attention, and the coal they mine counts as score.
- One coal fuels a drill for about 27 s, so 8 coal keeps a drill running about 3.5 minutes.

What paid best:
- Mass burner drills into chests on coal and iron. Our score roughly doubled in the last 5 minutes from these.
- Hand-mining coal earns about 1.5 points per second, so do it when idle.
- Unburned coal left in drills and chests still counts.

What wasted time:
- Clicking inventory slots, which was flaky.
- Rotating with R, which was unpredictable.
- Placing drills where they don't fit (the preview must be green), and walking around.
- Planning power and labs with no one assigned; it never happened.

Controls:
- Pipette is the reliable way to pick up an item: hover over a placed entity of that type and press Q.
- Wait 0.5 s between mouse_move, click and Q, or actions fail silently.
- Drills placed facing north; the output is above the top-left tile, so the chest goes about 14 px left and 45 px up of the drill centre.
- With coal in the cursor:
  - Ctrl+right-click on a drill inserts half the stack.
  - Ctrl+left-click inserts all of it.
- Hover a machine and zoom on the info panel (x 1088–1280, y 380–620) to see its fuel count.
- Chests: hover, click, then shift-click a slot to take the stack.
- E opens the inventory.
- Walking speed is about 6 tiles per second.

Team split and chat:
- One player smelts iron and crafts drills and furnaces.
- One player expands coal mining and keeps every drill fuelled.
- One player runs iron and copper drills with furnaces.
- One player handles research, or more drills if power isn't realistic.
- Announce what you place and where in short chat messages.
- Ask for coal when your drills are dry.
- Don't take coal from someone else's chest without saying so; my chest went empty unexpectedly.
- At about 16:00, make sure every drill has enough fuel to reach 20:00.
