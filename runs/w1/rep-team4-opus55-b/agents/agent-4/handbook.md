Notes from the previous team (final score 14,360)

MAP: Spawn is in the centre. Iron and copper are just NW of spawn (copper W, iron E, touching). Coal is far SE: about 12 s of diagonal walking (S+D), then 2 s S.

BIGGEST LESSON: Coal was the bottleneck. Drills and furnaces kept running out of fuel, and walking coal by hand wasted a lot of time.
- First 3–4 minutes: build burner drills on coal that feed each other or feed chests.
- Then belt coal toward the ore, or set up a steady coal-runner role.
- Nobody built power, so research stopped after Electronics. A steam engine early would have let labs and electric drills run.

WHAT SCORED:
- Every plate counts, so keep all furnaces fuelled.
- Hand-crafting red science (5 s each, from 1 copper and 1 gear) was a steady score source in the last 10 minutes.
- Hand-mining ore (hold right-click on ore) runs alongside crafting at about 12 ore per 30 s. Always mine while the craft queue runs.
- In the last 3 minutes, size the queue to finish before 20:00; unfinished crafts are wasted. Cancel the end of the queue (left-click a queue item) if it is too long.

CONTROLS:
- Opening entity GUIs is unreliable. Hover, wait 1 s, then click; if nothing opens, click a few pixels lower. Stand within about 3 tiles. Press Escape before opening another GUI.
- Shift-click moves whole stacks between inventory and entity.
- After "click stack, then click slot", check the cursor; it may still hold items.
- Crafting menu: E. Left-click queues 1, shift-click queues all possible. Ctrl-click did nothing.
- Chat: press the grave key (`), type, press Enter.

TEAM SPLIT:
- 1 player: coal mining and delivery from the start.
- 2 players: iron/copper drills and furnaces, kept fuelled.
- 1 player: early steam power and labs, then crafting science.

CHAT: State which chest holds what, and which drills are out of fuel. Say what you are taking so nobody makes a wasted trip to an empty chest.
