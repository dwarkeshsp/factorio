Notes from agent-1 (final team score 14,360 at 20:00)

Map:
- Spawn has a big lake to the east.
- Iron is NW of spawn, 2–3 screens away. Copper is right next to it on the west, and stone is north of both.
- Coal is a small patch S/SE of spawn, with forest on its south and east sides. Walk around through the open sand to the west; walking into the trees gets you stuck.

What to do first:
- Fuel was our bottleneck the whole game. Drills and furnaces sat idle with no coal.
- Early on, build a self-feeding coal setup:
  - Put one burner drill on the coal and a second drill directly north of it, both facing north.
  - The bottom drill feeds the top drill's fuel slot. The top drill outputs into a chest on its top-left tile.
- Fuel the bottom drill once; the top drill then needs nothing.
- Make many of these chains. Late in the game we had about 15 drills on coal, and the score rose fastest then (about 1,100 per minute).

What scored well:
- More burner drills, built as early as possible.
- Hand-mining coal: hold the right mouse button on the ore. It gives about 0.6 per second and is steady score while you wait, so never stand idle.
- One teammate crafted red science for score.

What wasted time:
- Nobody built a lab or queued research. Decide this in the first minute or skip it.
- Fumbling with the GUI and walking back and forth between patches.

Operating the game:
- E (inventory) often doesn't register. Left-click empty ground first, then press E, then check with a screenshot.
- Entity windows often need two clicks or a 2-second wait. Shift-click only works once the window is actually open.
- To place something: click the item in your inventory, press Escape, click the map, then press Q to clear the cursor.
- To refuel: hold coal on the cursor and ctrl+click the drill.
- Hovering over something shows its fuel and contents in the right panel.
- To walk, use hold_key for 0.3–1 seconds (about 9 tiles per second).
- Chat: press `, type the message, then press Enter.

Team split:
- One player on coal chains and refuelling.
- Two on iron/copper drills and furnaces.
- One crafting: drills first, then lab and science.
- Post short chat messages that say where you put shared items, for example "50 stone in E iron chest at coal".

**Notes from team 1 (final score 14,360 at 20:00)**

**Map.** Iron and copper are adjacent, NW of spawn (copper on the W side, iron on the E). Stone is N of spawn. Coal is far SE, about 12 s of diagonal walking from the iron, and surrounded by forest. A big lake lies NE of the coal.

**What scored.** Score grew mainly from the number of burner drills kept running.
- Drills dropping ore into stone furnaces (ore 3 pts, plate 6) were the biggest earner.
- Drills on coal feeding chests were the next biggest.
- From 13:00 to 20:00 the score went from about 7k to 14k as drill count grew. Build drills and furnaces as early and as continuously as possible.
- Hand mining is only about 1.5 pts/s. Do it only while waiting.

**What wasted time.**
- Fuel starvation was the biggest loss. Many drills sat with red no-fuel icons.
- One player should be a dedicated fueler who walks a loop, giving each drill and furnace about 10 coal.
- Coal drills pointed into each other (drill→drill chains) help.
- Power and labs never got built, and research was irrelevant to score in 20 minutes. Skip it.
- Walking between coal and iron is about 12 s each way. Carry big coal loads rather than many small trips.

**Controls.**
- E opens inventory. The production tab is in the crafting panel.
- After Escape, the first click on an entity selects it and the second opens its GUI. Wait about 1 s between them.
- Shift-click a stack in the GUI to auto-insert it. Right-click takes half a stack, then click the fuel slot.
- A red selection box means the target is out of reach (about 100 px). Walk closer.
- R rotates before placing; check the yellow arrow points into the furnace. Your own character can block placement.
- Clicks sometimes miss, so verify with a screenshot.
- Hand-mine with mouse_hold right for 30–60 s on a tile next to you, and check that its box is yellow.
- Watch that clicks don't open a nearby drill by accident.

**Team split.**
- 2 players: mine stone, craft furnaces and drills, and place them on iron and copper.
- 1 player: builds coal drills into chests.
- 1 player: fuels everything continuously.
- Announce in chat (grave key, type, Return) where chests and new unfuelled drills are. Say when you empty a chest so others don't walk to it.
- In the last minute, craft gears or drills from loose plates.
