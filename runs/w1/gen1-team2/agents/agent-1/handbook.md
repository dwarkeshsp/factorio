Final team score was 21,163. These notes are from agent-1, playing a 20-minute run.

MAP: Spawn is near water to the east. The iron patch is next to spawn. Copper is SW/W of the iron. Coal is about 50 tiles S/SE of the copper, which is a 12-second run (S+D) each way. There are trees around the coal.

FIRST 3 MIN: Hand-mine stone and a little iron. Craft stone furnaces and burner drills. Build a coal pinwheel first: 4 drills around one chest, then add N-facing drills that feed each other's fuel slots so they stay fuelled. Fuel was our bottleneck all game.

WHAT SCORED:
- Rows of a burner drill facing N with a stone furnace directly above it, on iron and copper. 12 iron pairs plus 6 copper pairs gave roughly 20–25 points/s late in the game.
- Hand-crafting plates into copper cable added value for free while I walked. Shift+click queues everything.
- Coal left unburned is worth 3 points, but a coal burned in a drill yields about 7 ore, so always burn it.
- Ore you mine by hand counts too.

WASTED TIME:
- Drills and furnaces ran out of fuel unnoticed; at the end 3 of my 6 copper drills were dry.
- Long coal round trips.
- Clicking entities that were out of reach, which silently does nothing.
- Hand-mining needs the cursor within about 3 tiles of your character. My 30-second hold at about 3 tiles away mined nothing.
- Trying to mine "rocks" that were only decoratives.
- Research did not matter.

CONTROLS:
- Build reach is about 300 px from the screen centre.
- The first click on an entity selects it; a second click opens it.
- Drill fuel slot is at about (667,396) and furnace fuel slot at about (667,434). With coal in the cursor, right-click a fuel slot to add 1.
- Ctrl+right-click an entity in the world inserts half the cursor stack; ctrl+left-click inserts all.
- Ctrl+click a furnace or chest with an empty cursor to empty it. Furnaces stop at 100 plates.
- Hover over an entity and read "Fuel:" in the panel on the right.
- Q clears the cursor. Q can also pipette the hovered entity, so watch what is in your hand.

TEAM:
- One player owns coal: build more coal drills early and carry coal to the rows every 2–3 minutes.
- The other builds and fuels the iron and copper rows and empties furnaces.
- Put about 15 coal in each drill and 8 in each furnace so they run 5+ minutes unattended.
- Post short chat updates on where you are and what needs fuel.
