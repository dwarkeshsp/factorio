Notes for the next team (final team score 21,163 at 20:00)

Map: iron ore is northwest of spawn, copper directly west of the iron, and coal about 15–20 s walk south of the iron. Dense trees lie south and east of the coal and you get stuck in them; reach the coal from its north or west side. There is a big lake east and southeast of spawn.

What scored best: burner drill → stone furnace pairs, with the drill facing north into the furnace above it. I ended with 12 such pairs on iron in two rows. Build pairs early and keep them fueled. Score climbed about 25–35 per second late in the game. Self-feeding coal drill pairs placed facing each other, or drills dropping into chests, supply the fuel. Handcrafting plates into iron gears adds a little value, and mined ore and coal count too.

Fuel math: a drill burns about 0.0375 coal/s and a furnace about 0.0225/s. Unburnt coal sitting in fuel slots still counts, so overfuel everything once around minute 14 and stop worrying. Run a fuel check around minutes 12–16: hover each machine and read "Fuel" in the right panel. A furnace sat dead for a while because I missed it.

Time wasters:
- Walking into trees.
- Hand-mining tiles that were out of reach; reach is only about 2.7 tiles, and a red bracket means too far.
- Escape opening the game menu when the furnace window hadn't actually opened.

Controls:
- The first click after opening or closing a window is often lost. Move the mouse, wait 0.4 s, then click.
- Ctrl+left-click a furnace with an empty cursor takes its plates; you can do all 12 in one batch.
- Ctrl+left-click inserts the whole cursor stack. Ctrl+right-click inserts half.
- In the crafting menu, right-click a recipe to craft 5, Shift+click to craft all.
- Only queue crafts that will finish before 20:00, since half-done queues may not count. Gears take 0.5 s each.
- Furnace output stops at 100 plates, so collect before then.

Teamwork: split by resource. One player handled iron pairs plus gears; the other handled copper pairs plus coal chests and fuel runs. Post short chat messages when you refuel or start a run. Don't research; it costs score time.
