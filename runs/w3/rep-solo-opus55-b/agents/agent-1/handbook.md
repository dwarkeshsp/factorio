Notes from the previous run (final score 5,387 at 20:00, from burner drills feeding stone furnaces plus hand-crafted gears)

MAP: Iron patch is NW of spawn with copper just west of it. Coal is SSE of spawn, about 10 s of walking from the iron. The lake is east. There is stone far to the NE; don't bother with it, the walk was a waste.

WHAT WORKED:
- Put burner drills on iron, each dropping straight into a stone furnace placed directly below it (press R so the drill faces south). With 7 drill/furnace pairs I got about 8–10 points/s late in the game.
- Two coal drills facing each other refuel each other forever. Set these up early.
- Turn spare plates into iron gear wheels by hand at the end. Gears are worth more than the plates, and crafting continues while you walk.

WHAT HURT:
- I built too few drills, too late; the 7th pair was only placed around 13:15. Build more drill/furnace pairs as early as possible.
- Fuel kept running out. A drill burns 1 coal per 27 s and a furnace 1 per 44 s. Give drills 10+ coal and furnaces 5+ each round, so you aren't constantly running back. Several of my drills sat idle for minutes.
- I queued gears too late. At 20:00 the queue still showed 51 gears unfinished, so those plates probably earned nothing extra. Start the final gear crafting by about 18:30, and only queue what can finish: each gear takes 0.5 s.

CONTROLS:
- **Open an entity:** mouse_move onto it, wait 1 s, then left_click.
- **Drill GUI:** fuel slot at (668,396); inventory row at y=237.
- **Furnace GUI:** fuel slot (668,432), output (937,390); inventory row at y=230.
- **Fueling:** left-click the coal stack to pick it up, right-click the fuel slot once per coal (0.3 s waits), then left-click back on the stack's slot. Check afterwards that the cursor is empty; coal stacks shift between slots.
- **Collecting plates:** shift-click the furnace output.
- **Crafting:** right-clicking a recipe queues 5. Intermediates tab is at (840,220), gear at (668,300).
- **Walking and reach:** 1 s of walking covers about 10 tiles (~320 px). Reach is about 10 tiles.
- **Menus:** Escape closes a GUI. With nothing open it brings up the game menu; Resume is at (639,290).
