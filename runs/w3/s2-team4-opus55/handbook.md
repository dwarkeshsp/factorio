Notes from agent-1 (team final score 12,208)

MAP: From spawn, copper is a short walk west (about 6 s of holding A), iron is south, stone is south-southwest next to the iron, and coal is north/northeast. There is a lake to the southeast and trees just northwest of the copper.

START INVENTORY: Each player has 1 burner mining drill, 1 stone furnace, 1 wood and 8 iron plates. The first two inventory icons look alike. Hover over them to check which is the drill and which is the furnace; I placed the wrong one first.

WHAT SCORED: Hand-mining was the steady earner. Holding the right mouse button on an ore tile gives about 0.5 ore/s, roughly 1.5 points/s. Do it in long holds of 120–235 s; one hold covered most of my game. I mined about 450 copper ore. Smelting your own ore adds value (plate about 6 vs ore 3). A fueled furnace with 6 coal took 50 of my ore. I don't know how the rest of the team score splits between hand-mining, drills and research.

WHAT WASTED TIME:
- Fuel was the bottleneck. Every drill and furnace sat idle for minutes waiting for coal.
- 1 wood runs a drill for only about 13 s.
- Next time, put 2 people on coal in the first minute. Set the drill on coal so it outputs into a chest or furnace as a buffer, and have one person run coal to the iron and copper drills.

UI TIPS:
- The chat line stays open after you type. Press Return to send, not "\n", or your later W/A/S/D keys get typed into chat.
- Esc closes the chat and other windows.
- Placing: open inventory (E), click the item, press E again, hover to see the preview, then left-click. A red bracket means out of reach, so move closer.
- A drill placed directly below a furnace, facing north, feeds into it.
- Shift-click an item in an open machine window to insert the stack.
- Clicking a machine once may only select it; click again to open it.

TEAM:
- Post your role in chat in the first 10 s.
- One player runs coal and fuel full-time.
- The other three each own an ore patch: drill, furnace, then nonstop hand-mining.
- Check chat (`) every few minutes for fuel requests.

Notes from agent-2. The team finished on 12,208.

**What to do first (0:00–3:00)**
- Hand-mine stone and iron, craft stone furnaces and burner drills, and get drill→furnace pairs running on iron.
- Place a drill facing north (the default), then a furnace one tile-pair (64 px) directly above it. The drill feeds the furnace directly, so no belts or inserters are needed.
- Iron is in the centre. Stone is directly west of the iron, coal about 12 s walk north, copper north-west, and a lake east.

**What earned the most score for the time**
- Burner drill + furnace pairs on iron, built early and kept fuelled. Every pair keeps adding plates all game.
- Huge rocks: about 9 s of holding right-click gives about 37 coal and 37 stone. Big rocks give about 20 stone in about 5 s.
  - They show as tan squares on the map (M).
  - Hover a rock first: if there is no tooltip, it is decoration and cannot be mined.
- Turning plates into drills adds value, and the drills then produce more.

**What wasted time**
- Fuel mistakes. Ctrl+left-click dumps the whole cursor stack, so 50 coal went into one furnace and 22 into one drill. Use ctrl+right-click to insert half, and always confirm with the hover tooltip.
- Crafting drills late with no coal or wood left to fuel them. Five drills sat unused in my inventory at the end.
- A drill whose output drops on bare ground stops after one item. It must feed a furnace or a chest.
- Chopping trees late for chest wood was slow and missed its targets.
- Research gave nothing in 20 minutes.

**Controls**
- Clicks are sometimes dropped, so check the result.
- Inventory (E): slot 1 is at (340,241), and slots are 30 px apart.
- Furnace GUI: fuel slot (667,432); shift-click the output slot (937,390) to take everything.
- Right-click a slot to take half.
- Building reach is about 10 tiles; mining reach is about 3 tiles.
- hold_key with W, A, S or D moves about 9 tiles per second and overshoots, so tap it for 0.3–0.5 s.
- Q clears the cursor. If Escape opens the game menu, click Resume.

**Team split**
- One player on coal, delivering it to everyone else's drills on a schedule.
- Two players on iron pairs, and one on copper and stone or rocks.
- A drill burns 1 coal per about 27 s and a furnace 1 per about 44 s, so 20 minutes needs a lot of coal. Coal was our bottleneck.
- Chat with the grave key (`): say where you are building and how much coal you need.
- In the last 3 minutes, empty every furnace, craft everything into higher-value items, and keep mining by hand until 20:00.

MAP

WHAT WORKED

- Coal was the bottleneck all game. Build coal drills early, have them feed each other or a furnace used as a buffer, and keep one person on coal logistics.
- Hand-mining ore by holding right-click on a tile gives about 0.7 ore/s. That is free score while you wait or craft, because crafting continues while you mine.
- Hand-craft spare plates into iron gear wheels (Intermediates tab, shift-click crafts them all). 135 gears take about 67 s.
- In the last 3 minutes: fuel everything, queue crafts, then just mine.

WHAT WASTED TIME
- Long walks to the coal patch and back, and chopping wood.
- Furnaces that sat with no fuel and drills stuck on "Target full". A stone furnace holds 100 plates at most, so empty the outputs.
- Pressing Escape with no GUI open opens the game menu, and that cost me about 45 s. Press E to close the inventory instead, or click the X.

CONTROLS
- Click entities directly. The first click after a GUI opens or closes, or after a mouse_hold, is often ignored, so use mouse_move, then wait 1, then click.
- To fuel something, open its GUI and shift-click the coal stack in your inventory; this is reliable. Dragging a stack to the fuel slot often failed silently, so zoom in to check.
- Right-click a stack to take half of it.
- Shift-click a furnace's output slot to take the plates.
- Reach is about 80 px on screen for mining and about 10 tiles for building.
- Hover an entity to see "No fuel" or "Working" in the right-hand panel.

TEAM SPLIT
1. One player on coal: drills plus buffer furnaces, and delivers coal.
2. Two players on iron and stone: drill-furnace pairs, and craft more drills and furnaces.
3. One player on copper plus research (red science), using gears from the others.

CHAT
- Use the grave key to open chat, type, then press Enter. Post short messages naming the place and what you need, e.g. "iron S: furnaces need 30 coal".
- Announce what you build so no one duplicates work.

Map: Coal is north of the start. Copper is about 8–9 s southwest of the coal. Iron is about 12 s south of the coal, with stone just west of it and a lake to the southeast.

What to do first:

- Late in our game the copper and iron drills ran out of coal. Fuel starvation was our biggest loss. Give every drill and furnace 15+ coal at once rather than 5 at a time.

What scored best for the time:
- Hand-mining ore is about 0.5/s, which is around 1.5 points/s, plus more if you smelt it. It is reliable and can be done any time.
- In the last 10 minutes I placed one stone furnace next to my mining spot and fed it hand-mined copper. That fitted alongside mining well.
- A teammate reported that huge rocks (tan squares on the map) give 37 coal and 37 stone for a few seconds of mining. Check the map early and use the real ones.

What wasted time:
- Small brown rocks near the copper are decoratives. They show no tooltip and cannot be mined. Hover first and walk only if a tooltip appears.
- Item drops between players failed. Hand items over through a chest or furnace instead.
- Coal buffers that nobody empties fill up at 50 and the drill stops.

Operating the screen:
- First clicks often fail. Move the mouse, wait 1 s, then click.
- Placing: open the inventory (E), click the item, close with E, hover, click, then press Q.
- Shift+click moves a whole stack. Right-click a stack takes half; click the target slot to put it down.
- In a furnace window, the ore slot is above the fuel slot and the output is on the right.
- Watch where your inventory slots shift, or you will put stone in the fuel slot.
- Mine by holding the right mouse button on ore, up to about 100 s per hold.
- Chat: press grave (`), type the message, then press Return as a separate key action. Never type "\n" into the message.

Team split:
- 1 person builds and fuels coal and keeps everything fed.
- 2 people mine and smelt iron and turn it into drills and furnaces early.
- 1 person handles copper and stone.
- Announce locations and fuel levels in chat.
