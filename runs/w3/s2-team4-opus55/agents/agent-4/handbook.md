Notes from a 4-agent team (final score 12,208 at 0:20:00)

Map: Coal is north of the start. Copper is about 8–9 s southwest of the coal. Iron is about 12 s south of the coal, with stone just west of it and a lake to the southeast.

What to do first:
- Put burner drills on coal, each facing a stone furnace or chest that collects its output. They run for about 10 minutes on roughly 30 coal.
- Have one person deliver fuel continuously.
- Late in our game the copper and iron drills ran out of coal. Fuel starvation was our biggest loss. Give every drill and furnace 15+ coal at once rather than 5 at a time.

What scored best for the time:
- Hand-mining ore is about 0.5/s, which is around 1.5 points/s, plus more if you smelt it. It is reliable and can be done any time.
- In the last 10 minutes I placed one stone furnace next to my mining spot and fed it hand-mined copper. That fitted alongside mining well.
- A teammate reported that huge rocks (tan squares on the map) give 37 coal and 37 stone for a few seconds of mining. Check the map early and use the real ones.

What wasted time:
- Small brown rocks near the copper are decoratives. They show no tooltip and cannot be mined. Hover first and walk only if a tooltip appears.
- Item drops between players failed. Hand items over through a chest or furnace instead.
- Coal buffers that nobody empties fill up at 50 and the drill stops.

Operating the screen:
- First clicks often fail. Move the mouse, wait 1 s, then click.
- Placing: open the inventory (E), click the item, close with E, hover, click, then press Q.
- Shift+click moves a whole stack. Right-click a stack takes half; click the target slot to put it down.
- In a furnace window, the ore slot is above the fuel slot and the output is on the right.
- Watch where your inventory slots shift, or you will put stone in the fuel slot.
- Mine by holding the right mouse button on ore, up to about 100 s per hold.
- Chat: press grave (`), type the message, then press Return as a separate key action. Never type "\n" into the message.

Team split:
- 1 person builds and fuels coal and keeps everything fed.
- 2 people mine and smelt iron and turn it into drills and furnaces early.
- 1 person handles copper and stone.
- Announce locations and fuel levels in chat.
