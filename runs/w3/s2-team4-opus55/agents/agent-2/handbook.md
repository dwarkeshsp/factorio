Notes from agent-2. The team finished on 12,208.

**What to do first (0:00–3:00)**
- Hand-mine stone and iron, craft stone furnaces and burner drills, and get drill→furnace pairs running on iron.
- Place a drill facing north (the default), then a furnace one tile-pair (64 px) directly above it. The drill feeds the furnace directly, so no belts or inserters are needed.
- Iron is in the centre. Stone is directly west of the iron, coal about 12 s walk north, copper north-west, and a lake east.

**What earned the most score for the time**
- Burner drill + furnace pairs on iron, built early and kept fuelled. Every pair keeps adding plates all game.
- Huge rocks: about 9 s of holding right-click gives about 37 coal and 37 stone. Big rocks give about 20 stone in about 5 s.
  - They show as tan squares on the map (M).
  - Hover a rock first: if there is no tooltip, it is decoration and cannot be mined.
- Turning plates into drills adds value, and the drills then produce more.

**What wasted time**
- Fuel mistakes. Ctrl+left-click dumps the whole cursor stack, so 50 coal went into one furnace and 22 into one drill. Use ctrl+right-click to insert half, and always confirm with the hover tooltip.
- Crafting drills late with no coal or wood left to fuel them. Five drills sat unused in my inventory at the end.
- A drill whose output drops on bare ground stops after one item. It must feed a furnace or a chest.
- Chopping trees late for chest wood was slow and missed its targets.
- Research gave nothing in 20 minutes.

**Controls**
- Clicks are sometimes dropped, so check the result.
- Inventory (E): slot 1 is at (340,241), and slots are 30 px apart.
- Furnace GUI: fuel slot (667,432); shift-click the output slot (937,390) to take everything.
- Right-click a slot to take half.
- Building reach is about 10 tiles; mining reach is about 3 tiles.
- hold_key with W, A, S or D moves about 9 tiles per second and overshoots, so tap it for 0.3–0.5 s.
- Q clears the cursor. If Escape opens the game menu, click Resume.

**Team split**
- One player on coal, delivering it to everyone else's drills on a schedule.
- Two players on iron pairs, and one on copper and stone or rocks.
- A drill burns 1 coal per about 27 s and a furnace 1 per about 44 s, so 20 minutes needs a lot of coal. Coal was our bottleneck.
- Chat with the grave key (`): say where you are building and how much coal you need.
- In the last 3 minutes, empty every furnace, craft everything into higher-value items, and keep mining by hand until 20:00.
