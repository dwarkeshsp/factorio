Notes from a team that scored 7,679 at 20:00 (two players, burner-only).

MAP: The iron patch is about 30–40 tiles NW of spawn. Copper is just W of iron and stone is NE of iron. Coal is about 60 tiles S/SE of spawn, at the edge of a forest. Walking 1 s covers about 10 tiles, so one trip between iron and coal takes about 14 s.

WHAT WORKED:
- Rows of burner drills, each outputting straight into a stone furnace (drill below, furnace above).
- Hand-mining ore all the time while waiting (about 0.45 ore/s, roughly 1.3 points/s). Score rose fastest in the last minutes, so never idle.

WHAT FAILED:
- Coal logistics. Coal drills feeding wooden chests needed constant hand refueling and hauling. Iron drills still ran out repeatedly, and three were out of fuel at the end.
- Build coal drills in facing pairs (each fuels the other) from the start, and put coal next to the iron line with a belt or chest.
- Better: put the iron line's fuel supply right next to it, or pick iron/coal spots that are close together.
- Do fuel runs early and big; 10 coal per drill lasts only about 4.5 minutes.

CONTROLS:
- Ctrl+left-click a chest with an empty cursor to take everything in it.
- To fuel things:
  1. Press E, click the coal stack to hold it, and press E again to close the inventory.
  2. Ctrl+right-click an entity to insert half the held stack, or left-click it to insert the whole stack.
  3. Hover over the entity afterwards to confirm the fuel count. It silently fails when you are out of reach (more than about 4 tiles), so walk closer.
- Press Q before clicking an entity to open it. Otherwise you insert into it instead.
- If an entity GUI is open, holding right-click does nothing. Close GUIs before long mining holds; I lost 30 s this way.
- Clicking the bottom of a furnace selects the drill below it; click near the top of the furnace.
- Pressing Escape with no GUI open opens the game menu; choose Resume. Never click Quit.
- Chat: press the grave key, type your message, then press Return as a separate key action.

TEAM SPLIT:
- One player builds and crafts drills and furnaces nonstop.
- The other mines coal and stone by hand early, then becomes the fueler/hauler.
- Post a short chat message on each handoff: what you fueled, how much coal, and where you are going next.
