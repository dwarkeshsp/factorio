Notes for the next team (our final score was 8,570 at 20:00).

FIRST 2 MINUTES: Hand-mine stone and craft stone furnaces. Craft burner drills from hand-mined iron. Find coal early, since everything burner-based depends on it. Iron and stone are south of spawn (stone just W/SW of the iron), copper is NW, the lake is NE, and coal is north.

WHAT PAID: Burner drills feeding a stone furnace directly above them. Each pair gives about 0.25 plates/s with no further handling. More pairs plus steady coal is the whole game. Hand-crafting gears from spare plates adds a little value at almost no cost, so queue them while walking.

BIGGEST LOSS: Coal starvation. Four coal lasts a burner drill only about 108 s, and a furnace burns 1 coal per about 44 s. My drills ran dry repeatedly while I ferried coal over a 12–14 s walk. Fix: early on, put several drills on coal that feed chests, or that feed each other's fuel slots, and refuel each drill with 8–10 coal at a time. In the last 3 minutes, about 5 coal per drill is enough.

WASTED TIME: Walking for coal, failed GUI clicks, and a boiler/steam build that added little before 20:00.

SCREEN OPERATION:
- To open a building, move the mouse onto it, wait 0.3 s, then click. Clicking without hovering first often fails.
- Reach is about 300 px from your character.
- Click the lower half of a drill; the upper part opens the furnace above it.
- To add N fuel: left-click the coal stack, right-click the fuel slot N times, then click the same inventory slot to put the rest back. Zoom in to verify, because stacks sometimes end up on the cursor.
- Shift-click a furnace output to take its plates. Shift-click ore from your inventory into a furnace's input.
- Press E for crafting. Shift-click a recipe to craft all of it.

TEAM SPLIT: One player owns coal production and research. The other owns the iron/stone smelting cluster. Agree early on a coal drop chest halfway between the two areas, and post short chat messages saying what you took and when you'll return.
