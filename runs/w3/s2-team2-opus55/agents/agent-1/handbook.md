Notes from team 1 (final score 8,570, all burner tech)

**Map**
- The coal patch is about 12 s walk north of the iron patch.
- Stone is on the W/SW edge of iron, a lake is east of iron, and copper is NW of iron.
- Avoid the dense forest to the W/NW.
- The small rocks near the coal are decorative and can't be mined for stone, so don't bother.

**What scored**
- The score climbed fastest once many burner drills were running. Ours went from about 4,200 at 14:00 to 8,570 at 20:00, mostly from drills plus hand-mining.
- Build drills early, aim for 15 or more, and build them before 10:00.
- Every drill should output into an iron chest (8 plates). We used boilers as coal buffers, but their fuel slot caps at 50 and then the drills feeding them stop. That cost us lots of output and constant babysitting.
- Pair iron drills with stone furnaces, as drill → furnace.
- Hand-mining coal near the end added roughly 0.5 coal/s per player. Keep it going when nothing else needs doing.
- Turn spare plates into gears near the end, since crafting adds value.

**Wasted time**
- Fuel ferrying between coal and iron.
- Walking in the wrong direction.
- Picking the wrong item from inventory, because the inventory order shifts after crafting.
- Research barely mattered.

**Controls that work**
- **Opening an entity:** hover, press and release the left mouse button, then wait about 1.5 s. The first click after a GUI opens is often dropped, so repeat it and verify with zoom.
- **GUI slots:** your inventory row 1 is at y=240, x=341, 371, 401… Drill fuel slot is (667,396), boiler fuel (667,357), chest slot 1 (667,357).
- **Moving items:** shift-click moves a whole stack. Right-click a stack to pick up half, then left-click a slot to drop it.
- **Crafting tabs:** the crafting tab icons are at y=220, with logistics at x=683, production at 760 and intermediates at 840.
- **Crafting clicks:** a click crafts 1, shift-click crafts 5. The screen lags, so check the queue at the bottom left before clicking again.
- **Placing and rotating:** R rotates. A drill's output tile is where its yellow arrow points; zoom on the ghost before placing.
- **Hover tooltips** show drill fuel without opening the GUI.

**Teamwork**
- Split the work: one player on iron (drills, furnaces, collecting plates), one on coal plus crafting drills.
- Iron player: leave plates and stone in a chest at the coal site for the coal player, so neither of you walks needlessly.
- Coal player: put coal in a chest the iron player passes by.
- Post short chat updates with numbers and times, e.g. "took 75 coal, back 18:00".
