Notes from a previous run (final score 8,823 at 20:00)

Map: The big iron patch has copper directly west of it. Stone is about 5 s of walking NE of iron. Coal is about 12 s SE of iron (hold S+D), with forest right beside it. Enemies are peaceful, and research isn't worth it.

What scored best:
- Burner drills on coal set up in facing pairs. Rotate one drill east and the other west with R while placing, side by side. Each fills the other's fuel slot, so a pair needs only about 3 starting coal and no chests. I found this late; do it early with many drills.
- Iron drills each feeding a stone furnace placed directly above them (a drill outputs from the tile above its left column).
- Plates crafted into gears at the end (shift-click to craft all).
- Hand-mining ore in idle seconds counts too.

Main mistake: drills and furnaces ran dry while I was away. A burner drill burns about 1 coal per 27 s and a furnace about 1 per 45 s. Load roughly 15–20 coal per drill early so you aren't walking back and forth between sites. My iron drills sat empty for a while, and that cost me a lot.

Operating the GUI:
- E opens the inventory. With a drill open, its fuel slot is at about (667,396); with a furnace open, at about (667,434). Shift-click the furnace output at (937,390) to take plates.
- To fuel: click your coal stack, right-click the fuel slot N times (1 coal each), then click the stack back into your inventory before closing.
- After pressing Escape, the first click on an entity often fails. Move the mouse over it, wait 1 s, then click. Check that the GUI really opened before clicking blind.
- If no GUI is open, Escape opens the game menu, and blind clicks can hit "Save game" or "Quit". This happened to me once and I opened the Save dialog by accident (I did not save). Take a screenshot when unsure.
- Hovering shows a tooltip on the right with the fuel count and status. Red fuel icons blink, so don't trust them at a glance.
- Interaction reach is about 10 tiles (about 300 px); a red outline means out of reach.
- Walking 1 s covers roughly 250–300 px, so it's easy to overshoot.
