Notes from agent-1:

Notes for the next player. My final score was 5,960; the end state was 7 burner drills feeding 7 stone furnaces on the iron patch.

**Map**
- The iron patch is right beside spawn.
- Coal is about 12 seconds' walk north (W key) of the iron.
- Stone is just southwest of the iron.
- Copper is about 60 tiles west. I never used it.

**What to do first**
1. Mine stone and coal by hand: right-click and hold on the resource.
2. Craft burner drills and stone furnaces from the Production tab.
3. Place each drill facing north with a furnace directly above it. The drill feeds the furnace with no inserter.
4. Put two drills facing each other on the coal patch so they fuel each other. Collect their coal often.

**What wasted the most score: fuel**
- A drill burns about 1 coal every 27 seconds; a furnace about 1 every 44 seconds.
- I kept topping things up with 3–5 coal, and they kept running dry.
- At 20:00, 4 furnaces and 2 drills were idle.
- Build a much bigger coal supply early (4 or more coal drills), and load each machine with 10–15 coal per round.

**Hand-mining is worth it**
- About 0.5 ore per second (about 1.5 points per second) when you have nothing else to do.
- I mined about 76 ore in the last 2.5 minutes.

**Other notes**
- Hand-crafting gears from plates adds a little value.
- Boilers I crafted early were never used. Don't make things you won't place.
- Research is pointless because there are no labs.

**Controls**
- E toggles windows. Escape with no window open brings up the game menu. If that happens, click Resume and never Quit.
- After walking, always hover the cursor on the entity and wait 1 second before clicking, or the click fails. If the window didn't open, the next E opens your inventory and your batched clicks go to the wrong place.
- Moving fuel: click the coal stack in your inventory, right-click the fuel slot once per coal, then click the inventory slot to return the rest.
- Shift-click moves a whole stack. Shift-click a recipe to craft all you can.
- Hover a drill to see its fuel count in the right-side panel. Zoom on it to read the number.
- A furnace with the red icon has no fuel.
- Reach is about 10 tiles. If the cursor turns red, walk closer.
