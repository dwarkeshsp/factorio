Notes from agent-2 (I scored 2,772; agent-1 scored 18,617, so follow agent-1's notes over mine)

**What went wrong.** I never got past a handful of burner machines. Fuel starvation stalled me for most of the run. I scored about 3 pts/s for 20 minutes. Agent-1's approach is far better.

**Map.** Iron is about 45 W and 40 N of spawn, with copper just W of it. Stone is about 4 s E and 2 s N of the iron, beside a dense forest that gives wood. Coal is about 12 s S and E of the iron.

**Do first.**
1. Get coal early, 50 or more. Burner drills and furnaces eat fuel constantly.
2. Place many drill-and-furnace pairs fast. A burner drill sits on ore facing a stone furnace, and the drill output feeds the furnace directly.
3. Keep the hand-craft queue busy the whole game, not only at the end.

**What I found.**
- Hand-mining pays about 1.5 pts/s (0.5 ore/s at 3 pts each), about the same as one drill-and-furnace pair. When you have nothing better to do, hold the right mouse button on ore.
- Wood is fuel at 2 MJ each, and a tree gives 4 wood in about 2 s. Mining trees gives roughly twice the energy per second of hand-mining coal.
- A furnace or drill fuel slot holds one fuel type only. Wood cannot be added to a slot that already holds coal.
- Idle furnaces keep their coal, so reclaim it with ctrl+click on the fuel slot.
- Check fuel on every machine each cycle. My drills silently ran dry for minutes at a time.

**What wasted time.** Walking between outposts, tiny fuel top-ups, and not stocking stone early for furnaces.

**Interface.**
- Always mouse_move onto the target before clicking.
- Mining is `mouse_hold` with the right button, with the cursor within about 80 px of the character.
- E opens the inventory only with the mouse over empty ground, around (300,600). The inventory toggle is flaky, so check screenshots.
- Ctrl+click on a furnace output slot (937,390) takes all the plates. Ctrl+click on an inventory slot while a machine GUI is open moves the stack into the machine.
- Right-click an inventory slot to pick up half the stack.
- In crafting, left-click queues 1 and right-click queues 5.
- Never press Escape.
