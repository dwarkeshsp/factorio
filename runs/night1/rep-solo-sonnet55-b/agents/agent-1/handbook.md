Notes for the next player (my final score was 1,998):

**Controls (this cost me about 90 seconds)**
- Left-click and left mouse hold do NOT mine. Mining is the RIGHT mouse button held over a resource. Use mouse_hold with button "right".
- Reach is short. A red cursor bracket means the ore is out of reach, and a yellow bracket means it is in reach. Stand on the patch and aim the cursor within about 1–2 tiles of the character, e.g. (610,360) when the character is at (640,340).
- Check inventory with E. You start with a burner mining drill, 1 wood and 8 iron plates, and no pickaxe in the bar.
- mouse_hold allows up to 300 seconds, so one call can cover a long stretch.

**Map**
- Walking W+A for 8 seconds from the spawn put me directly on a large copper patch. Iron is right next to it to the southeast. Stone is to the north, coal to the south, and water to the east.

**What I did**
- I hand-mined copper continuously at about 0.5 ore/s (roughly 1.5 points/s). That gave about 2,000 points in 18 minutes. I did nothing else, and that was too passive.

**What to do better**
1. Start mining by hand at once. Never leave the character idle.
2. Place the burner drill on ore in the first minute and fuel it with the wood. Put a wooden chest or a second drill in front of it so its output isn't blocked. Drills keep producing while you mine by hand.
3. Use your hand-mined iron and stone to craft more burner drills (3 gears, 3 plates and a stone furnace each). Also mine coal as fuel. Each drill adds about 0.25 ore/s, which is about half my hand rate. Ten drills by minute 8 would greatly outproduce hand mining.
4. Smelting doubles value, since a plate is worth about 6 and an ore about 3. Feed drills into furnaces, but only once raw ore is flowing.
5. Don't spend time on research or on long builds that won't pay back before 20:00. Keep hand mining between crafting sessions.
