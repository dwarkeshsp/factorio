Notes for the next red player (final: red 3,277; blue 26,135; green 4,294; yellow 543)

What to do first:
- Iron ore is NW of the start, with copper directly W of it. Coal is far S/SSE, next to a lake on its NE side. Stone is about 25 tiles E of the iron patch.
- The other teams grab spots fast, so claim iron and coal tiles in the first minutes.
- Build burner drills dropping straight into stone furnaces (drill faces down, furnace directly below). This was my main score source.
- On coal, place two burner drills facing each other so they fuel each other. Coal piles up in their fuel slots, so walk over and take it.

Scoring lessons:
- Blue won by building many drill→furnace pairs early. Build more machines sooner instead of hand-mining.
- Every extra drill+furnace pair is worth more than anything you can do by hand. Hand-mining gives only about 0.4 ore/s.
- Coal left sitting in fuel slots still counts toward score; only burned coal is subtracted. So overfuel freely.
- Coal trips took me about 40 s each way. Pre-load each drill with about 15 coal and each furnace with about 10 so they run unattended.

Operating the game:
- To pick a buildable into the cursor, hover an existing building of the same type and press Q (pipette). Picking from the inventory slot and then pressing E often lost the item.
- Rotate with R and zoom in to check the ghost's arrow before placing.
- To fuel: open an entity (hover, wait 1 s, left-click). The drill fuel slot is at (667,397). Right-click it to take half into the cursor.
- With coal in the cursor, ctrl+right-click an entity to insert half, or ctrl+left-click to insert all.
- DANGER with Escape: if the GUI didn't actually open, Escape opens the game menu, and my next clicks pressed Quit (I was put back in). Close GUIs with E or the X button, and take a screenshot first.
- Walking: about 7–9 tiles per second. Press M to see the map, because the minimap is confusing.
- Last minute: craft iron gear wheels from leftover plates (Intermediate tab, shift-click to craft all).
