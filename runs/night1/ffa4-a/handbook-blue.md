Notes from blue-1:

Blue won 26,135 to 4,294. Build burner drills early: a drill working for ten minutes beats a last-minute crafting sprint.

Start on iron. Aim the starting drill’s yellow output arrow directly into a stone furnace. Fuel both; use wood until you fetch coal. Collect plates and reinvest them in drills. Hand-mine stone for furnaces. Copper is immediately west of iron; coal is southeast, about ten seconds’ diagonal walk from the southern iron row. Stone is northeast of iron. Carry enough fuel and stone per trip.

Best expansion: on coal, place two drills facing each other, arrows pointing into each other. Seed with coal; they mine straight into their partner’s fuel slot. Repeat in pairs. We ran 32 coal drills. Sweep out their accumulated coal periodically so full slots don’t stop production; leave or restore seed fuel. On iron and copper, use one furnace per drill, no belts or inserters. We reached 14 iron and 7 copper pairs before the final expansion. Queue steam engines from surplus iron while walking and building. Machine output scores immediately; don’t collect plates unless you need them or the furnace is full. Skip electricity, labs, and late long-distance trips.

Hotbar: 1 drill, 2 furnace, 3 coal. R rotates; check the arrow before placing. Q clears your hand. Ctrl-left-click with coal selected fuels a machine; with empty hand it takes output (or drill fuel!). At our zoom, adjacent machine centers were 35 pixels apart. Drag-place rows, then inspect: fast drags skip machines. Move the pointer onto a missed target before clicking; repeat if needed. Hover for “Working” or “No fuel”; flashing alerts disappear between blinks. E opens crafting; right-click queues five, shift-left queues maximum. Keep handcrafting through the deadline.
