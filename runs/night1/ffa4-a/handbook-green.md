NOTES FOR NEXT PLAYER (I finished 2nd with 4,294; blue won with 26,135 using about 20+ drill/furnace pairs on the iron patch)

**What scores**
- Burner drill facing a stone furnace on the iron patch, both coal-fuelled. Each pair gives steady points. I had only 5 pairs; build 20+ like blue did.
- Crafting plates into iron gear wheels raised the score noticeably. Shift+click the gear recipe (third crafting tab) to queue all; it runs while you hand-mine. Collect plates and do this regularly, not just at the end.
- Hand-mine with one long right mouse_hold (60 s) whenever idle.

**Order**
1. Place the starting drill and furnace on iron immediately. Hand-mine stone (stone patch ~25 tiles N and ~25 E of the iron) for furnaces.
2. Reinvest every plate into more burner drills and furnaces until about 12:00. Then stop building and convert plates to gears.
3. Coal is ~70 tiles S/SE of the iron. Put 2–4 burner drills there feeding a chest early and fetch in bulk. Long coal walks were my biggest time waste. Wood works as early fuel.
4. Fuel use: drill 1 coal per 27 s, furnace 1 per 44 s. Before the final 3 minutes give each drill ≥7 and each furnace ≥4.
5. Ignore research.

**Operating**
- Time passes only during actions. The first action in a batch is often dropped, so start with a mouse_move or wait. Put wait 1 after every click or key.
- e and clicks that open a building often fail the first time. Screenshot to confirm before clicking inside a window.
- With the inventory closed and an item in hand: ctrl+right-click a machine inserts half the stack, ctrl+left-click inserts all. With an empty hand, ctrl+left-click takes the output.
- In a machine window, right-click a slot to take half.
- Drills place facing north. Press r before placing and check the yellow arrow points at the furnace.
- Walking is ~9 tiles/s and hold_key takes whole seconds. A 2x2 building is 64 px.
- q clears the hand. Don't press it while hovering a machine.
- Other teams' buildings sit right beside yours; don't click them.
