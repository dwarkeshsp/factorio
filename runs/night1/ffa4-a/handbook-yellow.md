Notes for the next player (I finished last: yellow 543, red 3,277, green 4,294, blue 26,135)

What went wrong
- I spent almost the whole game hand-mining coal at one patch, about 30-40 points per minute. I never found iron or stone and never built anything, so I had no way to grow.
- Blue, red and green covered the same coal patch with dozens of burner mining drills. Their drills sat on top of the spot I was mining. Several of my long right-click holds probably hit a drill or empty ground and produced nothing, and I only looked at the screen every 5-8 minutes. Take a screenshot every 30-60 seconds of mining to confirm the count is still rising.
- I mapped once at the start. The map showed no ore icons, only uncharted checkered areas.

Where things are
- You start in open desert. The coal field is a short walk east and south, roughly 5-6 seconds of walking.
- The map at the start shows blue water to the east and the other teams' start areas to the northwest.
- I never found iron, copper or stone. Look for them first, by walking in several directions and checking the minimap, or the map with M, for ore colours.

What I would do
1. In the first 2 minutes, find iron and stone. Hand-mine stone and iron ore by holding the right mouse button on it. Craft a stone furnace (5 stone) and a burner mining drill (3 gears, 3 plates, 1 furnace).
2. Build burner drills on iron and coal and feed them coal. Drills that run while you do other things are what produce the score. Early on, nothing beats a drill producing while you are away.
3. Put iron into furnaces and keep expanding drills. Smelted plates are worth about 6 points each, double raw ore.
4. Avoid waiting for the whole game on hand-mined raw ore.

Controls
- Batch several actions in one call.
- hold_key W/A/S/D walks. Walking ~1 second moves a few tiles.
- mouse_hold with the right button on a resource mines it.
- The game keeps running while you think about long holds, so keep holds to 30-60 seconds and check.
