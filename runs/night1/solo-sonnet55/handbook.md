Notes for the next player (final score 1,370; the game is 20 minutes long)

**What the score rewards.** Raw ore is worth about 3 each, a plate about 6, and crafted items more. Spent ingredients stop counting. Hand-mined and hand-crafted output counts the same as machine output. Mining rocks and chopping trees give nothing, and wood is only useful as fuel.

**What to do first.**
- Wood was my bottleneck all game. Burner drills and stone furnaces both burn it, and 2 wood lasts a drill only about 26 s.
- Chop a large stack of wood (about 40 or more) before building anything.
- The dense forest is SW of the iron patch. I left it too late, then judged the walk too long and never went back.
- Coal is far south and stone far north, so I never used either. Wood was the only fuel I used.
- Iron is NW of the start. Copper lies directly west of it and I never needed it.
- Mine iron ore by hand early. Use a furnace for plates, and plates are worth about twice the ore.

**What earned the most per minute.**
- Hand-mining and smelting kept the score climbing steadily.
- Burner drills with chests worked, but only while fuelled. Mine sat idle for several minutes at a time.
- Crafting spare plates into iron gear wheels was a good late bonus. It is on the Intermediate tab, takes 0.5 s each, and adds roughly 5 points per gear. It runs in the background while you do other things.

**What wasted time.**
- Letting the drills run dry and then trekking back to refuel.
- Clicking GUIs from too far away.
- E-key desyncs that left the wrong window open.
- Walking to the forest and back. A round trip took about 25 s.
- I never found stone furnaces in the crafting menu. Don't count on crafting them from stone.

**How to operate the game.**
- Walking: `hold_key` w/a/s/d moves about 250 px per second.
- Mining and chopping: `mouse_move` onto the target, wait 1 s, then `mouse_hold` right for 3–4 s. Reach is only about 3 tiles.
- Opening a chest or furnace: the first click often only selects it, so click twice with a 1 s wait in between.
- Moving items: shift-click moves a whole stack. ctrl-click moved all but one in a furnace slot.
- Splitting a stack: right-click picks up half.
- Crafting: click an item once to queue it. Shift-click queues 5 and ctrl-click did not queue anything.
- Pressing E can desync, so check a screenshot after each press.
- Never press Escape with no window open. It opens the pause menu.
