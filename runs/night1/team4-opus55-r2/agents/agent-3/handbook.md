Notes for the next team (final score was 14,960; it grew from about 400/min at 9:00 to about 1,500/min by the end).

**Map**
- Spawn is in the centre.
- Copper is NW of spawn, and the iron patch touches it on its east side.
- Coal is far south/SE.
- Stone is north.
- A lake lies east of spawn.
- A second iron patch is about 150 tiles north.

**What to do first**
- Coal is the bottleneck for everything. In the first 2 minutes, send one player to coal to hand-mine and build burner drills feeding chests.
- Build a shared iron chest at the iron/copper border as a fuel and material drop point.
- Late game, many burner drills and furnaces sat at "No fuel" while ore lay unsmelted. Assign one player full-time to refuelling every few minutes.

**What paid off**
- Hand-mining ore near the furnaces: about 0.4 ore/s, about 3 points each, no coal needed.
- Smelting that ore: a stone furnace takes 3.2 s per plate and about 1 coal per 12–14 ore, so it roughly doubles the value.
- Burner drills only pay if someone keeps them fuelled.

**What wasted time**
- Walking between distant patches.
- Leaving a drill and furnace pair at copper with no coal supply.
- Unclear chest ownership: my coal was taken by others.

**Controls**
- Clicking a building often only shows its tooltip. Click again after a 1-second wait to open the GUI.
- Shift-click of ore into a furnace did not work for me. Click the ore stack to pick it up, then click the furnace input slot.
- Coal: shift-click from the inventory fills the fuel slot. Right-clicking to split stacks was unreliable.
- When finished, put the cursor stack back in an inventory slot or press Q before Escape, otherwise the held stack is not where you expect.
- Mining: hold the right mouse button on an ore tile within about 9 tiles (the cursor turns yellow). Hold for 60–120 s at a time.
- Chat: press the grave key (`), type, then press Return.

**Team split and chat**
- 1: coal miner and fuel ferrier.
- 2: iron drills and furnaces.
- 3: copper/iron hand-mining plus smelting.
- 4: stone and furnace crafting, then a second smelting line.
- Everyone works within 30 tiles of the iron/copper border.
- Post short chat messages with exact chest locations and how much fuel each site needs.
