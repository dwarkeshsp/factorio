Notes from agent-1 (final team score 14,960 at 20:00)

Map: spawn is near the centre. Iron is about 25-30 tiles NW, with copper right next to it on the west side. Stone is north of spawn and a lake is east. Coal is about 60-80 tiles S/SE; from the iron field, walk S+D for about 5s, then S for 4s. Another iron patch is about 150 tiles north and has no coal nearby, so avoid it.

What to do first: hand-mine stone and iron, craft burner drills and stone furnaces, and put a drill-into-furnace pair on iron immediately. Coal is the bottleneck: every burner drill and furnace starves without it. One player should build 4-6 burner drills on coal early, feeding chests, and the others haul coal from there. Each drill burns about 1 coal every 27s, so 10-15 coal keeps one running for 5-7 minutes. Most of our score came from many burner drills running continuously plus hand-mining. Fuel left unburnt still counts, so overfilling is fine.

What wasted time:
- Long walks between distant fields.
- Drills sitting idle without fuel.
- Taking plates out of chests that teammates had already earmarked.
- Researching with nothing to use it for. Steam power and Electronics got us little in 20 minutes.

Controls:
- The first click on an entity after Escape often fails. Click twice with about 1s between clicks.
- To split a stack, right-click your inventory stack to pick up half, then left-click the target slot. Shift-click moves a whole stack.
- Burner drill fuel slot is at about (667,396). Furnace output slot is at (937,390); shift-click it to take everything.
- Place items one at a time: select, wait 1s, move the mouse, wait 1s, click.
- Mining reach is about 2.5 tiles. Hand-mine with a long right-button hold (45-120s).
- Hover over an entity to see its tooltip "Fuel: N".
- For chat, press `, wait 1s, type, then press Return.

Team split: 1 player on coal drills and fuel delivery, 2 on iron and copper drill/furnace lines, and 1 crafting more drills and furnaces. Announce in chat what you are taking from shared chests and which drills you have fueled.
