Notes from agent-4. Our team's final score was 14,960.

**What earned score**
- Burner mining drill plus stone furnace pairs on iron ore, with each furnace placed directly on the drill's output side (north of the drill). These ran unattended and were the main score engine.
- Hand-crafting gears from plates near the end added a bit, and so did crafting furnaces from stone. Crafted items count even if they sit unused in your inventory.

**What hurt or wasted time**
- Coal was the real limit, not ore. My iron patch was far from coal, and drills kept running dry.
  - Build drills on the coal patch first, with drills feeding each other's fuel and some feeding a chest.
  - Then haul about 50 coal per trip, or build the iron setup next to coal.
  - Without enough coal, crafting more drills is mostly wasted: I ended holding 48 unplaced burner drills.
- Fuel use: a burner drill burns about 1 coal per 27 s and a furnace about 1 per 44 s. Budget for that.
- Long walks cost a lot of time. Stay compact and near coal.
- Big rocks give only 20 stone. Huge rocks also give coal, so they are worth mining early.

**How to operate the screen**
- Left-click an entity with an empty hand to open it.
  - Drills overlap the furnace above them, so click near the top of the furnace sprite.
- Inserting from your hand:
  - Ctrl+left-click inserts ALL of that item from your inventory.
  - Ctrl+right-click inserts half of it.
  - To split fuel, keep only a small amount in total.
- Ctrl+left-click a furnace with an empty hand to take its plates.
- Fuel reach is about 10 tiles. A red outline under the cursor means you are out of reach.
- Hover an entity and zoom on the right-hand tooltip panel to read fuel counts.
- Mining a rock: hold the right mouse button; it sometimes needs two holds.
- In the crafting menu, right-click a recipe to queue 5 and shift-click to queue all.
- Stop queuing crafts that won't finish before the deadline.

**Team split**
- One agent: coal drills plus coal delivery. One agent: research and power.
- Two agents: drill and furnace arrays on iron and copper next to coal.
- Announce locations and coal needs in chat early. Ask for coal deliveries instead of each agent foraging alone.
