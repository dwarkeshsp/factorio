Notes for the next team (our final score was 14,960 at 20:00)

**Map**
- Iron ore is NW of spawn, copper west of the iron, stone north of the iron.
- Coal is south/south-east of spawn. A lake is east.
- Huge rocks sit just north of the iron field. Mining one by hand takes about 6 s and gives about 37 stone and 37 coal, which is the best early stone and coal source.

**What scored**
- Burner drill and stone furnace pairs on iron ore: the drill faces north, and the furnace is centred about 3 tiles directly above the drill.
  - Crafting the drills and furnaces counts as score too.
  - I built mine only after 15:00. Starting by about 5:00 and adding pairs continuously would have multiplied the score.
- Ore supply was our bottleneck. Several furnaces sat idle waiting for hand-fed ore.
  - Build a drill for every furnace.
  - Do not hand-mine iron to feed furnaces, except in the last minute.
- In the last 2 minutes, convert spare plates into gears or burner drills, then hand-mine ore until the end. Mining and crafting run at the same time.

**What wasted time**
- Ferrying coal back and forth between areas. Instead, put coal drills feeding chests, and collect from the chests rarely.
- Research: nobody made science, so it didn't matter.
- A full furnace output (100 plates) stops the furnace, so empty outputs with ctrl+click.

**Fuel**
- A burner drill burns about 1 coal every 27 s; a furnace about 1 every 45 s.
- Don't overfuel near the end, because burnt coal stops counting.

**Controls (the GUI lags)**
- Clicks and the `e` key often do not register the first time. Wait 2 s after each GUI action and zoom in to verify.
- The placement ghost often doesn't render. Click anyway, then check that the building appeared.
- Build reach is about 8 tiles, so walk closer if a click does nothing.
- To fuel: open the building, click the coal stack to pick it up, right-click the fuel slot once per coal to drop one at a time, then click the stack back into your inventory.
- Hold the right mouse button on a rock or ore to mine it.

**Team split**
- 1 player: coal drills into chests, keeping a public coal chest at the iron field.
- 2 players: build iron drill and furnace pairs (rock stone plus plates).
- 1 player: copper pairs and crafting.
- Chat briefly with locations and needs, e.g. "coal chest east of iron furnaces, 50 coal".
- Keep everyone within the same area. A player 150 tiles away contributed little.
