Notes from agent-1 (final team score 14,960 at 20:00)

Map: spawn is near the centre. Iron is about 25-30 tiles NW, with copper right next to it on the west side. Stone is north of spawn and a lake is east. Coal is about 60-80 tiles S/SE; from the iron field, walk S+D for about 5s, then S for 4s. Another iron patch is about 150 tiles north and has no coal nearby, so avoid it.

What to do first: hand-mine stone and iron, craft burner drills and stone furnaces, and put a drill-into-furnace pair on iron immediately. Coal is the bottleneck: every burner drill and furnace starves without it. One player should build 4-6 burner drills on coal early, feeding chests, and the others haul coal from there. Each drill burns about 1 coal every 27s, so 10-15 coal keeps one running for 5-7 minutes. Most of our score came from many burner drills running continuously plus hand-mining. Fuel left unburnt still counts, so overfilling is fine.

What wasted time:
- Long walks between distant fields.
- Drills sitting idle without fuel.
- Taking plates out of chests that teammates had already earmarked.
- Researching with nothing to use it for. Steam power and Electronics got us little in 20 minutes.

Controls:
- The first click on an entity after Escape often fails. Click twice with about 1s between clicks.
- To split a stack, right-click your inventory stack to pick up half, then left-click the target slot. Shift-click moves a whole stack.
- Burner drill fuel slot is at about (667,396). Furnace output slot is at (937,390); shift-click it to take everything.
- Place items one at a time: select, wait 1s, move the mouse, wait 1s, click.
- Mining reach is about 2.5 tiles. Hand-mine with a long right-button hold (45-120s).
- Hover over an entity to see its tooltip "Fuel: N".
- For chat, press `, wait 1s, type, then press Return.

Team split: 1 player on coal drills and fuel delivery, 2 on iron and copper drill/furnace lines, and 1 crafting more drills and furnaces. Announce in chat what you are taking from shared chests and which drills you have fueled.

**What scored**
- Burner drill and stone furnace pairs on iron ore: the drill faces north, and the furnace is centred about 3 tiles directly above the drill.
  - Crafting the drills and furnaces counts as score too.
  - I built mine only after 15:00. Starting by about 5:00 and adding pairs continuously would have multiplied the score.
- Ore supply was our bottleneck. Several furnaces sat idle waiting for hand-fed ore.
  - Build a drill for every furnace.
  - Do not hand-mine iron to feed furnaces, except in the last minute.
- In the last 2 minutes, convert spare plates into gears or burner drills, then hand-mine ore until the end. Mining and crafting run at the same time.

**What wasted time**
- Ferrying coal back and forth between areas. Instead, put coal drills feeding chests, and collect from the chests rarely.
- Research: nobody made science, so it didn't matter.
- A full furnace output (100 plates) stops the furnace, so empty outputs with ctrl+click.

**Fuel**
- A burner drill burns about 1 coal every 27 s; a furnace about 1 every 45 s.
- Don't overfuel near the end, because burnt coal stops counting.

**Controls (the GUI lags)**
- Clicks and the `e` key often do not register the first time. Wait 2 s after each GUI action and zoom in to verify.
- The placement ghost often doesn't render. Click anyway, then check that the building appeared.
- Build reach is about 8 tiles, so walk closer if a click does nothing.
- To fuel: open the building, click the coal stack to pick it up, right-click the fuel slot once per coal to drop one at a time, then click the stack back into your inventory.
- Hold the right mouse button on a rock or ore to mine it.

**Team split**
- 1 player: coal drills into chests, keeping a public coal chest at the iron field.
- 2 players: build iron drill and furnace pairs (rock stone plus plates).
- 1 player: copper pairs and crafting.
- Chat briefly with locations and needs, e.g. "coal chest east of iron furnaces, 50 coal".
- Keep everyone within the same area. A player 150 tiles away contributed little.

- Late game, many burner drills and furnaces sat at "No fuel" while ore lay unsmelted. Assign one player full-time to refuelling every few minutes.

**What paid off**
- Hand-mining ore near the furnaces: about 0.4 ore/s, about 3 points each, no coal needed.

- Burner drills only pay if someone keeps them fuelled.

**What wasted time**
- Walking between distant patches.
- Leaving a drill and furnace pair at copper with no coal supply.
- Unclear chest ownership: my coal was taken by others.

**Controls**
- Clicking a building often only shows its tooltip. Click again after a 1-second wait to open the GUI.
- Shift-click of ore into a furnace did not work for me. Click the ore stack to pick it up, then click the furnace input slot.
- Coal: shift-click from the inventory fills the fuel slot. Right-clicking to split stacks was unreliable.
- When finished, put the cursor stack back in an inventory slot or press Q before Escape, otherwise the held stack is not where you expect.
- Mining: hold the right mouse button on an ore tile within about 9 tiles (the cursor turns yellow). Hold for 60–120 s at a time.
- Chat: press the grave key (`), type, then press Return.

Notes from agent-4:

Notes from agent-4. Our team's final score was 14,960.

**What earned score**
- Burner mining drill plus stone furnace pairs on iron ore, with each furnace placed directly on the drill's output side (north of the drill). These ran unattended and were the main score engine.
- Hand-crafting gears from plates near the end added a bit, and so did crafting furnaces from stone. Crafted items count even if they sit unused in your inventory.

**What hurt or wasted time**
- Coal was the real limit, not ore. My iron patch was far from coal, and drills kept running dry.
  - Build drills on the coal patch first, with drills feeding each other's fuel and some feeding a chest.
  - Then haul about 50 coal per trip, or build the iron setup next to coal.
  - Without enough coal, crafting more drills is mostly wasted: I ended holding 48 unplaced burner drills.
- Fuel use: a burner drill burns about 1 coal per 27 s and a furnace about 1 per 44 s. Budget for that.
- Long walks cost a lot of time. Stay compact and near coal.
- Big rocks give only 20 stone. Huge rocks also give coal, so they are worth mining early.

**How to operate the screen**
- Left-click an entity with an empty hand to open it.
  - Drills overlap the furnace above them, so click near the top of the furnace sprite.
- Inserting from your hand:
  - Ctrl+left-click inserts ALL of that item from your inventory.
  - Ctrl+right-click inserts half of it.
  - To split fuel, keep only a small amount in total.
- Ctrl+left-click a furnace with an empty hand to take its plates.
- Fuel reach is about 10 tiles. A red outline under the cursor means you are out of reach.
- Hover an entity and zoom on the right-hand tooltip panel to read fuel counts.
- Mining a rock: hold the right mouse button; it sometimes needs two holds.
- In the crafting menu, right-click a recipe to queue 5 and shift-click to queue all.
- Stop queuing crafts that won't finish before the deadline.

**Team split**
- One agent: coal drills plus coal delivery. One agent: research and power.
- Two agents: drill and furnace arrays on iron and copper next to coal.
- Announce locations and coal needs in chat early. Ask for coal deliveries instead of each agent foraging alone.
