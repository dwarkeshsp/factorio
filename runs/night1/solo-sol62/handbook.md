Notes from agent-1:

Benchmark: 18,785 at 20:00. Start a burner economy immediately. Iron is northwest of spawn, copper just west of iron, stone northeast beside the forest. Coal is southeast: from the gap between the original iron rows, walk about 2.8 seconds south, then 8.7 seconds southeast to the forest edge. Leave peaceful enemies alone.

Bootstrap iron with burner drills feeding stone furnaces directly. Put the furnace against the drill’s yellow output arrow (48 pixels center-to-center at my zoom). Reinvest early plates and stone in more pairs; build them early so they produce for minutes. At coal, place drills in face-to-face pairs and seed them with fuel. They feed each other; periodically collect their accumulated coal and refuel the factory in batches. Stone drills feeding iron chests are cheap additional score. A strong layout was 27 iron pairs, 3 copper pairs, 10 coal drills, and 3 stone drills.

Keep handcrafting while walking and building. Queue machines first, then bulk iron gears with surplus plates. Research, steam infrastructure, and hauling output without a reason weren’t worth the time. Output counts in machine slots and chests; collect only to prevent backups or get materials. Fuel in a slot isn’t deducted until burned.

Bind coal, burner drills, and chests to 1–3. Alt shows outputs, R rotates, and Q with an empty cursor copies a hovered building if you have one. Drag to place rows; inspect for skipped spots and player collisions. With coal held, Ctrl-left-drag fuels a row, but can spend 50 per machine; Ctrl-right gives half the held stack. Move the cursor onto the target before Ctrl-right-clicking: quick clicks sometimes hit the previous target. Empty-cursor Ctrl-left collects output but strips fuel from ore drills. Watch for blinking red fuel icons. Don’t leave expansion until the final minute.
