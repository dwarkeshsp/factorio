Operating:
- Always mouse_move to the target before clicking. Rapid repeated clicks get partly dropped.
- Number keys for the hotbar are unreliable; click the hotbar slot instead. Q clears the hand.
- Coal in hand + ctrl+right-click on a machine inserts half the stack (25 if you hold 50). Empty hand + ctrl+right-click on a drill takes half back. For furnaces, open the GUI and ctrl+right-click the fuel slot to take half.
- E opens crafting only if the mouse is over empty ground (move to 300,600 first). In crafting, left-click = 1, right-click = 5.
- Drills only place on ore; check the arrow faces the furnace, hover and press R to rotate.
- Hover a machine to read its fuel in the right panel.
- Never left-click an entity with an item in hand near menus, never press Escape; quitting ends the run.
