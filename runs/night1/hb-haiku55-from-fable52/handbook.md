NOTES FOR NEXT PLAYER (I scored 887)

What to do first:
- Don't spend early minutes on far-off setups. The coal-side chest with two burner drills I built produced almost nothing. Skip it.
- Start with hand-mining iron ore right away. The iron patch is north-east of spawn, and hand-mining earned about 4 points per second with no fuel needed. Ore counts as produced when mined, so you don't need a furnace to score it.
- Build burner drill plus stone furnace pairs on iron early, but expect fuel to be the limit. Wood is the only fuel nearby.

What earned most:
- Hand-mining ore (right-click hold on a blue-rock tile, 25-30 s per hold). It was the steadiest income from 15 to 20 minutes.
- Harvesting trees for wood, roughly 4 wood per tree in about 9 s, for drill and furnace fuel. A pair runs only about 30 s per tree, so this is weaker than it sounds.

What wasted time:
- Walking between the drill pairs and trees. Trees were often out of reach, so the harvest did nothing. Stand within about 2 tiles.
- Fueling drills by hand with 6 wood at a time. They ran dry in under a minute, and I was never able to keep three pairs running.
- Not getting coal. Coal is about 60 east and 70 south of the iron patch. A stock of coal would have fixed the fuel problem early, and I should have gone for it.
- Crafting almost nothing. Plates and gears were not made into anything useful.

Operating tips:
- Move the mouse to the target, wait about 1 s, then click. Fast repeated clicks get dropped.
- Hotbar: click the slot icon. Number keys were unreliable.
- Ctrl+click with a fuel stack in hand inserts it into a machine. Ctrl+right-click inserts about half.
- Check whether a tree was actually harvested by zooming on the hotbar. Wood appears in a slot without a count badge.
- Never press Escape or quit. Don't use the console.
