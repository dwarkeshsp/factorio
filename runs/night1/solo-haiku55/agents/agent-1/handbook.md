NOTES FOR THE NEXT PLAYER

Result: final score 932 at 0:20:00. Hand-mining coal was the best late earner. Most of the game was spent on logistics that did not pay back.

What to do first:
- Start the coal supply immediately. Place a burner drill on the coal patch (south-east of the iron, past the tree belt) with an iron chest directly on its output tile. Chest center is about 35 px above the drill center. Test with the drill window: "Target full" means the output is blocked, so the chest is in the wrong tile.
- Mine huge rocks by hand early for stone (about 20-30 each).
- Build iron drill and furnace pairs only after coal is flowing.

What earned the most per minute:
- Hand-mining coal rocks at the coal patch with mouse_hold right, 8 seconds per hold. This gave about 15 to 20 points per hold late in the game, roughly 90 points per minute. Alternate between 2 or 3 rocks, since a rock depletes.
- Producing plates in furnaces counts too, but needed coal to keep running.

What wasted time:
- Repeated trouble placing the coal drill's chest and the spare chest. I dropped an item on the ground and spent about 2 minutes picking it up.
- Rotating the drill with R did not fix its output. Position the chest, not the drill.
- Walking back and forth between iron and coal (about 50 tiles each way).
- Feeding coal to stone furnaces when the drill fuel ran out.

Operating tips:
- Check the held item on the cursor before clicking. Items on the cursor are drawn at the mouse position. A chest or coal on the cursor can look like it is on the ground.
- Left-click an entity to open its window. mouse_hold on an entity may open its window rather than mine it.
- Furnace ore slot is top-left, fuel slot is bottom-left, output is right.
- Keep batches short when moving items.
