We lost 7,544 to 34,635: blue had about 15 burner drills on coal and rows of drill+furnace pairs by minute 10; we had about 8 machines. Machine count early is everything.

**First 5 minutes**
- Place your starting burner drill on iron with a stone furnace on its output tile, and fuel both. Hand-mine coal, then stone (5 per furnace).
- Every 9 plates + 5 stone becomes another burner drill (Production tab). Keep the craft queue full.
- Put drills on coal outputting into wooden chests (2 wood each) early. Coal is 3 points each with no smelting, and it fuels everything.
- Stop building new drills around 16:00; they no longer pay back.

**What paid**
- Drill+furnace pairs running unattended.
- Fuelling generously once: about 14 coal per drill and 9 per furnace lasts 6 minutes. Over-fuelling costs nothing.
- Turning spare plates into belts: shift+click the recipe crafts all while you walk.

**What wasted time**
- Under-fuelling (1–3 coal), which left my iron row idle for minutes.
- Hand-mining, only about 0.25 items/s.
- Walking between patches, about 12 s each way.
- Trying to fuel blue's buildings ("Cannot transfer to enemy structures"). Hover first and check "Last user".
- Getting stuck between building rows. Leave walking gaps.

**Operating**
- Inputs get dropped under lag. Press E, then screenshot to confirm the inventory is really open before clicking slots.
- Stack in hand: ctrl+left-click on a machine inserts all, ctrl+right-click inserts half. With an empty hand, ctrl+left-click takes the output. Q clears the hand.
- Move the mouse onto the target and wait 1 s before clicking.
- Reach is about 10 tiles for buildings but under 3 for mining.
- Nothing under the right-hand panel (x>1088) is clickable.
- R rotates only with the item in hand. Hover a placed drill to check its yellow output arrow.

**Team**
- Chat at second 0 (grave key). One player takes iron and stone and builds pairs. The other takes coal: drills into chests, then ferries 50–100 coal to the smelting rows every 2 minutes.
- Build rows adjacent so one coal delivery reaches everything. We never talked, and it cost us.
