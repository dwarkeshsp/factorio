Blue won 34,635–7,544 with burner drills and stone furnaces. Build producers early: an extra drill working for ten minutes beats a last-minute mining sprint. Don’t race to electricity or research for its own sake.

Split immediately. Player A owns coal (southeast of iron) and iron expansion. Player B starts iron for drill parts, then develops copper just west of iron and stone east/northeast by the forest. Use grave (`), type, Enter for team chat. Claim rows and agree where coal goes: “I’ll fuel both iron rows; you take copper and stone.” Call out shortages, not every action.

On coal, put two touching drills facing each other; seed one with fuel. Replicate in rows. They feed each other but stall at 50 coal: harvest periodically. On iron/copper, point each drill directly into a touching furnace; fuel BOTH. Make straight rows for fast servicing. On stone, point drills into chests. Reinvest early plates and stone in drills and furnaces. Collect plates for crafting or to clear full outputs; moving stock alone earns nothing.

Use hotbar 1 drill, 2 furnace, 3 coal. Enable ALT, inspect yellow output arrows, rotate with R before placement. Q clears hand. Empty-hand Ctrl-left drag collects across a row; Ctrl-right takes half. With coal held, Ctrl-right inserts half a hand stack. Watch floating counts: drag can skip machines, and clicks sometimes land at the previous cursor position. Move first, then click. Leave walking lanes.

Keep handcrafting while walking and servicing. Late, turn spare iron into iron chests: E → Logistics → shift-left iron chest (8 plates per half-second). Don’t queue a huge lab batch near deadline: all intermediates craft first. Shift-left the final queue icon to cancel; completed intermediates remain.
