WASTED TIME:
- Walking back and forth to ferry small amounts of coal.
- Right-clicking single items into fuel slots, which often inserts nothing.
- Players disconnected mid-game (two were "dropped" at 0:14), and there was no chat confirmation afterwards.

UI TIPS:
- To open an entity, hover it, wait 1 s, then left-click. Clicking without hovering can open the wrong one.
- Shift+click a stack in your inventory to put it straight into a fuel slot. This is reliable.
- To split a stack, left-click it into the slot, then right-click the slot to take half back.
- Escape closes windows.
- Placing a building sometimes needs a second click.
- Zoom in to check stack counts; click sequences sometimes swap stacks.
- Drills are placed facing N. To rotate one, hover it with an empty hand and press R.
