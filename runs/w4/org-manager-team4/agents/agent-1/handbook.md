Notes from the previous team (final score 12,792)

Map: Iron is NW of spawn, about 14 s walk (8 s W+A, then 3 s S+D). Copper adjoins iron on its west side. Stone and a forest are north of iron. Coal is far south, 30+ s from iron. A lake lies east/SE of spawn.

What scored:
- Score was slow early: only 3,800 by 10:48. It climbed to about 20 points/s near the end once roughly 15 burner drill + stone furnace pairs had fuel.
- So build drill/furnace pairs as early as possible: drill facing north into a furnace. Expansion is what multiplies score.
- Hand-mining iron (about 0.37 ore/s) is a steady filler while waiting. Ore in inventory counts.

Main mistake: fuel, which means coal.
- For long stretches most drills and furnaces showed the red no-fuel icon.
- Send one player to coal in the first minute to build several coal drills chained into a chest, and ferry coal every 2 minutes.
- Wood (about 2–3 per furnace) runs out fast.

Wasted time:
- Hand-mining with the cursor out of reach. A red cursor means nothing is being mined; we lost a full minute that way.
- Shuttling small item batches between players.
- Hand-crafting gears, which adds little.

Controls through the screen:
- Plain left-clicks on world entities often fail. Use a left mouse hold of about 0.2 s to open an entity GUI.
- To load a furnace or drill: open its GUI, then shift-click the stack in your inventory (first slot about (340,232)). Or, with a stack in the cursor, click the furnace input slot at about (667,388).
- To mine, hold the right button on a tile 1–2 tiles from your character for 60–90 s at a time.
- Screenshots lag by one action, so wait 1 s before deciding something failed.
- Pressing Escape with no GUI open opens the game menu. Click Resume and never click Quit.
- Chat: press `, type, then press Return as a separate key action.

Team split (4 players):
- One on coal: drills plus ferrying coal.
- One on stone, making furnaces.
- Two on iron: building pairs, fuelling, hand-mining.
- Expand onto copper by mid-game.
- Manager states roles in chat at 0:00. Players report briefly what they placed. Use few messages.
