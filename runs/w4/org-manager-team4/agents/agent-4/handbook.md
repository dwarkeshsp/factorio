Notes from agent-4 (our team finished at 12,792)

MAP: Iron is NW of spawn, and copper touches its west edge. Stone is about 15–20 tiles NE of iron. The only coal we found is far SE, about 100 tiles E and 120 tiles S of iron, just SW of the lake. Walking there with S+D takes about 14 s.

WHAT SCORED: The score grew fastest once many burner drill+furnace pairs were running on iron. Fuel was always the limit: furnaces and drills kept going out (red icon). Build coal drills early and point them into a shared iron chest; four drills around one chest works well. One person should only carry coal: fill up at the coal chests, walk to iron, refuel everything red, and repeat every 2–3 minutes. Burning coal is worth it, because one coal makes a drill mine about 7 ore. In the last 3 minutes, hand-mining iron ore (hold right mouse on ore) added about 1,500 points. Don't stand idle at the end.

WASTED TIME: Long walks with little coal. Fiddly fuel insertion. Leftover items like boilers that never got used.

CONTROLS:
- Ctrl+right-click a building with a stack in hand inserts half of it.
- Ctrl+left-click puts in the whole stack. It then keeps pulling more coal from your inventory, so I accidentally put 50 coal into one drill.
- Clicks after E, Escape or opening a window often get eaten. Move the mouse first, wait 1 s, then click, and check with a screenshot.
- Shift-click a chest slot to take the whole stack.
- Red highlight on a building means it is out of reach; walk closer.
- Red icons blink, so look twice.

TEAM: The manager should give each person one job: coal mining, coal ferrying, building iron pairs, or crafting drills. Report in chat whenever you take coal from someone's chest or fuel something. I failed to say that, and we refueled the same buildings twice while others ran dry. Chat: grave key, wait 1 s, type, Enter.
