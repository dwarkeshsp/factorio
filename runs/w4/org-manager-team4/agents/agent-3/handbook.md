Notes for the next team (final score 12,792):

**Map**
- Iron is NW of spawn, with copper on its west edge.
- Stone is NE of the iron, with a dense forest north of the stone.
- Coal is S/SE of spawn. A lake lies to the east.

**What earned the most score**
- Burner drill feeding a stone furnace (drill faces north into the furnace above it), fuelled with coal.
- The score climbed fastest after about minute 14, when roughly 15 pairs were running. Build pairs as early as possible and keep building them.
- Every plate goes back into drills: 9 plates plus 1 furnace make 1 drill.

**First 5 minutes**
- Hand-mine stone, then craft furnaces.
- Put 2–3 burner drills on coal, outputting into a chest or into each other.
- Then build iron pairs nonstop.

**What wasted time**
- Fuel and ore were the bottleneck, not furnaces.
  - I placed 5 furnaces that relied on hand-fed ore. One sat idle with 50 finished plates and no ingredients.
  - A furnace without a drill feeding it is nearly useless.
- Chopping wood (4 per tree, about 1.3 s each, plus long walks) was poor value compared with coal drills.
- Spread-out solo projects (a stone-brick drill) ran out of fuel unnoticed.
- Research hardly matters for the score.

**Operating the game**
- Clicks get dropped often. Move the mouse, wait 0.4–0.6 s, then click.
- After each step, zoom on the window to confirm it worked.
- Inventory slots start at (340,232) and are 30 px apart.
- Production crafting tab: burner drill at (667,300), furnace at (667,330). Shift-click a recipe to craft the maximum.
- In machine windows:
  - Drill fuel slot is at (668,396).
  - Furnace input is at (667,388), fuel at (667,433), output at (937,390).
  - Shift-click a stack to move all of it.
  - To split fuel: pick up the stack, right-click the fuel slot once per item, then click the inventory slot to return the rest.
- Right-clicking a fuel slot with an empty hand pulls half the fuel out. Don't do it by accident.
- Build reach is about 8 tiles; a red bracket means too far.
- Chat: press the grave key, type, then press Return as a separate action.

**Team split**
- One player keeps coal drills fed and carries coal to every machine on a loop. This is critical: red no-fuel icons were everywhere.
- Two players build drill/furnace pairs on iron, using the plates.
- One player does stone, furnaces, and copper pairs.
- Report counts and locations in chat. Announce when a chest has coal or plates.
