Notes from agent-2 (fuel role). Our team finished at 12,392.

Map:
- Iron is NW of spawn, with copper adjoining it on the W/NW side.
- Coal is SE of spawn, about 20–25 s of walking from the iron.
- Stone is near the iron.
- Press M for the map; walking takes longer than you expect.

What to do first:
- Put self-feeding burner drills on coal right away. Either chain them (one drill drops into the next one's fuel slot) or have two drills face each other.
- Put 3–4 more coal drills dropping into a chest.
- Coal was our bottleneck the whole game. By the end, iron drills sat idle with no fuel while I walked back and forth with too little coal.
- Make a coal depot chest at the iron patch early and keep it stocked.
- The best value for time was more burner drill and stone furnace pairs on iron, kept fuelled.
- Hand-mining iron ore with the right mouse button held, plus crafting gears from spare plates, added steady score in the last minutes.

Mistakes that wasted time:
- After Electronics finished, nobody started the next research for about 10 minutes. Name one person to keep research running.
- Walking was slow and I kept getting lost; check the minimap after each walk.
- Ctrl+click on a furnace with an EMPTY hand takes its plates out instead of fuelling it. I did this by accident several times.

Fuelling through the screen:
1. Press E and left-click the coal stack so it is in your cursor.
   - Zoom in to confirm it is really in the cursor; my pickups silently failed twice.
2. Press E again to close the inventory.
3. Move the mouse over the drill or furnace, wait about 0.4 s, then:
   - ctrl+right-click inserts half of what you hold;
   - ctrl+left-click inserts all of it.
4. Press Q to empty the cursor.

Other controls:
- Hover over an entity to see its fuel and status in the panel at the right.
- A red outline on hover means the entity is out of reach.
- Taking from a chest: left-click the stack in the chest, then left-click an empty inventory slot.
- Chat: press the grave key, type, then press Return.

How to split the work:
- One person on coal and fuel.
- One on iron and smelting.
- One crafting drills and furnaces.
- One placing machines and doing research.
- The fuel person should be told whenever new drills are placed. Post short chat messages that say where things are and how many items.
