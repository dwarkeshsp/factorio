Notes from agent-4 (builder). Our team finished with 12,392 points.

WHAT SCORED
- Score came from burner drills feeding stone furnaces placed directly below them: drill faces south, furnace under its output.
- Each pair makes plates on its own for as long as it has fuel. Build these pairs early, many of them, on iron and copper.
- Hand-mining ore also counts but is slow. Do it only while nothing else is waiting.
- In the last 10 minutes the score rose about 10–20 points per second, almost all from fuelled pairs.

WHAT WASTED TIME
- Fuel ran out repeatedly. When a drill or furnace showed a red "no fuel" icon, its output stopped.
- One person must run coal nonstop. Give each machine 5 or more coal, not 1–2. One coal lasts a drill about 27 s and a furnace about 44 s.
- A drill feeding an unfuelled furnace stalls with "Target full".
- Nobody built a lab, so research never ran even though two techs finished. Only build one if the team commits to making red science early. Otherwise skip research.
- Crafting boilers and steam gear we never used was waste.
- Rocks north of the iron/copper patches are worth mining. A huge rock gives about 37 coal and 30 stone in about 8 s, and a big rock gives about 20 stone in about 5 s.

UI TIPS (1280x720)
- Clicking machines: move the mouse, wait 1 s, then click. The first click often only shows the tooltip, so retry.
- Fuelling: open the machine, left-click the coal stack in your inventory, then right-click the fuel slot once per coal with a 1 s wait between clicks. Fast right-clicks get dropped. Click the inventory slot again to put the rest back.
- Mining: mouse_move to the target, wait, then hold the right button with no coordinate.
- Placing: press e to close the inventory, r to rotate, then click. Check the screenshot, because placements sometimes fail silently.

TEAM SPLIT
- Two players craft drills and furnaces and place pairs.
- One player only mines coal and refuels.
- One player mines rocks for stone and coal.
- Say exact locations in chat. Check the chat often; requests went unanswered for us.
