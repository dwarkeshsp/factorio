Notes from agent-3 (final team score 12,392 at 20:00)

Map: stone is north of spawn. Iron is northwest. Copper is directly west of iron and touches it. Coal is south-southeast. A large lake is east. A huge rock north of the iron patch gives about 37 stone and 37 coal when mined.

What to do first:
- Hand-mine stone, craft stone furnaces, and get burner drills down on iron and copper as early as possible.
- Each drill should feed straight into a furnace: place the drill facing north and put the furnace directly above it, 2 tiles (about 64 px) up.
- Two drills can feed one furnace, one from above and one from the side.

What paid off most:
- Drill-and-furnace pairs. Every placed machine also counts toward the score.
- Hand-mining ore during dead time, about 0.4 ore per second at 3 points each.
- Queuing hand-crafts that run while you walk or mine. For example, shift+click circuits and then cable in the Intermediate tab.

What wasted time and lost output:
- Underfuelling. A burner drill burns one coal about every 27 s, and a furnace about every 44 s. Give each drill about 20 coal, not 7; mine went dry with 2 minutes left.
- One coal carrier was not enough. Agent-2 ran dry, and new drills sat unfuelled for minutes.
- One drill was placed badly and never used.
- Research stalled: Electronics finished and no Lab was ever built.

Operating the game through the screen:
- Pressing E sometimes does nothing. Wait 2 s and check the screenshot.
- The first click on a building often only shows a tooltip, so click again.
- Inventory slots shift as stacks empty, so re-check slot positions before clicking.
- Fastest way to fuel: left-click the coal stack, left-click the fuel slot (the whole stack goes in), then right-click the slot to take half back.
  - Right-clicking one item at a time often fails.
- Hover a placed drill and press R to rotate it. Q clears the cursor.
- Ctrl+click a chest or furnace to take its contents.

Team:

- Agree on fixed chest locations: a coal depot and a plate chest.
- Post short chat messages giving the place and what is needed, e.g. "3 drills at W copper need coal".
- Someone should build a Lab and red science by about minute 10.
