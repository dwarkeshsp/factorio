The final team score was 12,392. Notes for the next team:

**Map**
- Iron patch is NW of spawn, with copper on its W/NW edge.
- Stone patch is NE of iron, with forest north of it.
- Coal patch is far SE/S, about 12 s walk from iron, with forest on its east side.
- A lake lies east.
- Rocks just SW of iron cannot be mined.

**What to do first**
- Make coal self-sustaining early: two burner drills on coal facing each other fuel each other.
- Coal piles up to 50 in each drill's fuel slot. Harvest it often; it was our best coal source.
- Fuel and stone were the bottlenecks all game, not plates. We ran out of coal at about 15:00 and machines sat idle.
- Put a second player on stone early. Hand-mining stone gives only about 0.35/s; I needed 60 s for 20 stone, which is enough for just 2 drill+furnace pairs.

**What scored best**
- Burner drill feeding straight into a stone furnace (plates are worth twice ore), placed as early as possible.
- Spare plates crafted into gears add value.
- Hand-mining ore in idle moments adds steadily.
- Fuel left in a fuel slot still counts, so overfilling a slot costs nothing.

**What wasted time**
- Walking: 1 s moves about 300 px on screen. Use 0.5–1 s holds and check the map (M) often.
- A furnace that switches ore type jams with "Output full" until you empty its output.
- In the last minute I held 25 coal while two drills sat unfuelled. Sweep every machine for red fuel icons from 18:30 on.

**Controls**
- Clicking a building often only selects it; click again to open its window.
- Shift+click moves a whole stack into the open building. Shift+right-click moves half the stack.
- Shift+click a recipe to craft all you can.
- Placing: R rotates the item on the cursor before placing (twice for south).
- Close windows with their X. Escape with nothing open brings up the game menu, which has Quit; never click Quit.
- E sometimes fails right after chat, so press it again.
- Chat: grave key, type, then press Return as a separate key action.

- One player on stone, delivering furnaces to a shared chest.
- One on iron and plates.
