- **Fuel first.** Astra hand-mined 16 coal before placing anything; Opus started on one wood. At minute 7.2 Astra had 6 drills and 164 coal, Opus 5 drills and 25 coal, and Opus's drills kept running dry.
- **Turn use.** Opus took 12.3 turns per game minute at 6.6 actions per turn; Astra 7.9 and 3.0. Each action costs about 0.3 s (my estimate), so Opus spent about 16 of its 40.5 minutes on action overhead and 5 more in 758 waits. 393 of its turns ended with a redundant screenshot request.

- **Transfers.** Astra swept furnaces with ctrl-click (185 by 40.5). Opus fed fuel one piece per right-click (406 of its 500).
- **Confusion.** About 95 Opus turns reason about being out of reach, stuck or lost (approximate keyword count).

## 2. Interactions

- **Ship.** Astra emptied it 4 s in (turn 4, "+8 Firearm magazine", shot 00004). Worthless under peaceful.
- **Same rock.** At minute 1.4–1.5 both mined one big rock side by side (Opus shot 00023). Astra got it: Opus's shot 00024 shows "+20 Stone (20)" while Opus stayed at 40.
- **Same patches.** Both used the single iron, copper, coal and stone patches, drills interleaved (Opus shot 00403). I found no sign of depletion.
- **Lake.** Astra built power at the lake at 8.7; Opus went to "the big lake to the east, where agent-2 has its things" and built just south at 19.7.
- **Building space.** Opus dropped a coal drill site (turn 419) and a belt smelter plan on iron (turn 443) because Astra's buildings left no room.
- **Boxed in.** Turns 424–427, minute 36.0–36.3: about 20 s and three failed walks. One wall was Astra's burner-drill pair ("Last user: agent-2", Astra shot 00296); the rest were Opus's own drills and chest, built against it (Opus shot 00426). Its longer jam (46 s, turns 204–211) was all its own buildings.
- **Attempted take.** Astra turns 407–408 (minute 52.8) ctrl-clicked Opus's two stone chests (33 and 114 stone). The game answered "Cannot transfer from enemy structures" (shot 00408). No reasoning was recorded, so intent is unknown.
- **Leak.** Opus read Astra's stock from floating text over Astra's furnaces ("+75 Iron plate (206)", shot 00135).
- **Misattribution.** After a compaction dropped it, Opus called its own first stone drill and chest "probably agent-2's" (turn 371).

No evidence of chat, console attempts, the shoot key, mining or attacking the other's buildings, deliberate blocking, or Opus's buildings obstructing Astra.

## 3. Strategy

Both played solo games. Opus mentions agent-2 in 46 turns, mostly to sort out ownership. The closest to racing is "Agent-2 has 206 iron plates and is ahead" (turn 136) and "Agent-2 already has a base with assemblers, so I need my own" (turn 179); neither changed its plan. Astra refers to Opus twice: "my competitor's poles are unusable" (turn 278) and "Those stone chests belong to the other side" (turn 409). Nobody claimed land early or spread out to deny it.

## 4. Fairness

- **Start.** Same spot (Astra shot 00003). Opus drilled iron first (0.47 against 1.25); Astra took most drill sites on the small iron patch by building more, sooner.
- **Turn budget.** It did not decide the result: Astra reached rung 12 at 39.7, before Opus's last turn. It does reward long actions.
- **Astra's functions.** It used `click` 29 times and `press_keys` 22 times, but never alongside a hold: every function call was the only call in its reply, and 243 turns were a lone hold. The harness sends no setting forbidding several calls, so I infer the model chose this. At turn 376 it bound the middle button to build and walk together as a workaround.
- **Astra's compaction** (turns 149, 292, 434) cost two screenshot-only turns and one blind 0.9 s walk.
- **Action errors.** None logged or skipped on either side.

## 5. For a competitive design

- Make something scarce; sharing cost nothing here.
- Budget game time, or stop both sides together.
- Show each side the other's progress; Opus learned it was behind from a leaked number.
- Mark ownership on screen: look-alike interleaved buildings cost Opus turns without creating a contest.
- Reward intermediate firsts or contested objectives; "launch first" was too remote to shape play.
