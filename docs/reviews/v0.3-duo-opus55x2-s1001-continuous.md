# Opus 5.5 pair, continuous schedule (v0.3) vs solo (v0.2), seed 1001

**Headline:** at equal game time the pair made about twice the solo's output from 10 to 35 min and 0.9–1.7× at 45 min, for 1.9× the money; at equal cost it made 0.3–0.5×. The continuous schedule removed the idle waiting. Both runs ended with nearly the same factory, the pair 6–16 min sooner. These are single runs: the direction is plausible, the sizes are not established.

Statements are verified from logs or screenshots unless marked "(est.)" or "(inferred)". Scripts are in `/tmp/review-duo3/`.

## 2. Idle time
Nobody waited. With per-action costs fitted on the solo run, each agent's unexplained game time is 3.7% and 3.6%: typing chat (169 s) and 0.045 s per teammate pause (39 s). Turns per game minute were 12.6 and 10.5 (solo 10.6; rounds 8.8).

A different gap appeared: agent-1 spent its 500 turns by 39.7 min and was absent for the last 7.7 min (16%).

## 3. Division of labour and chat
The split was agreed by 0.14 min and kept (turn ranges approximate).
- **Agent-1 (iron, copper, research):** t1–113 nine burner drill-furnace pairs; t114–176 lab, engine, pump; t204–292 pole line and science block; t336–426 ten electric drills; t445–500 more labs and assemblers.
- **Agent-2 (coal, stone, fuel, steam):** t1–115 five coal drills and deliveries; t116–142 boiler; t162–180 three copper pairs; t217–284 courier loop; t347–446 a 97-belt coal line to the boiler.

Of 57 chat attempts, 54 arrived. About 14 changed the receiver's behaviour (inferred): for example, 17.1 "build 4 more… on copper" gave three pairs by 19.0. Nineteen of agent-1's 27 asked for coal.

The opening deadlock did not recur, by luck: both again claimed iron at turn 2, but agent-2's message was never sent (see 7), and it accepted agent-1's plan next turn.

## 4. Costs of acting asynchronously
- **Role-crossing:** agent-1 made two coal runs on agent-2's job (t177–197, t309–331; 44 turns). In the first, both fuelled the same row, and agent-2 took agent-1's coal for its own failed click (t155–158).
- **World changed:** agent-1 took 177 coal from agent-2's chests (t316–318); 31 s later agent-2 found 18 (t253).
- **Same job:** both set out to add engine #2 (agent-1 t427–444).
- **Stale beliefs:** agent-2 announced "3 electric drills" (33.0 min) that were steam engines. After 39.7 min it messaged the finished agent-1 twice and left research empty ("agent-1 should pick one", t475).
- **Faded chat:** messages vanish after about 10 game seconds (est.). Four of 53 were gone from the receiver's next screenshot (agent-1 t258, t341; agent-2 t160, t243). "Boiler has 49 coal" (20.7) went unseen; agent-1 asked "boiler out of coal?" at 22.1.
- **Turns lost (est.):** agent-1 about 47 (9%), agent-2 about 37 (7%), plus the 44 above.

## 5. Gains
- **Parallel build:** pump, boiler and engine went down between 13.2 and 14.5 min; solo power came at 21.1.
- **Error catching:** agent-2 diagnosed the unpowered science block from "only 400W demand" (22.3; fixed 23.2).

## 6. Better or bigger?
Neither: the same factory, sooner. Placed, team v solo: burner drills 18 v 18, electric 10 v 11, furnaces 19 v 16, assemblers 5 v 2, labs 3 v 2, working engines 2 v 2, belts 97 v 222. Both hand-fed their furnaces. Over the last 7.7 min: iron 109 v 102 per min, copper 34 v 51, packs 8.6 v 6.0. The pair left 153 belts unused.

## 7. Harness
- **Clean:** no action errors (0 of 6,392), no desync in the logs, at least 56.5 ups on every span of 1 s or more.
- **Held keys:** pure hold-and-wait batches gain 0.7–2.3 ticks per pause (a third of a tile at most).
- **One unexplained spell:** at agent-1 t219–222 (17.8 min) the pointer was dead: four identical screenshots, two right-button holds mining nothing. It began where a pause fell at the release of a right-button hold (inferred); six other such coincidences did no harm.
- **Chat failed at first try 4 of 57 times:** three "\n" endings, which `type` does not send as Enter (agent-2 t2, t256; agent-1 t95), and one message typed into the game as hotkeys (agent-1 t296).
- **Silent no-op:** `screenshot` with a `region` returned the full frame (152 calls).

## 8. For the benchmark
- Keep the continuous schedule, but cap game time or pooled turns, not turns per agent, and tell a team when a member stops.
- Report all three comparisons; here they give about 2×, 1.2× and 0.3×.
- Solo variance is as large as the team effect: run several of each arm, and a 1,000-turn solo.
- Deliver chat as text: 8% of messages faded unseen.
- Log each input against pause ticks, and production per agent.
