# Opus 5.5 duo vs solo, seed 1001

**Headline:** the pair ended with 2.1–2.6× the solo's output only because 500 team rounds bought 1.6× the game time and 1.92× the money. At equal game time, equal spend or equal total decisions the pair was behind, at 0.3–0.75× the solo.

Unlabelled statements come directly from logs or screenshots; "(est.)" and "(inferred)" mark the rest.

## 1. Division of labour
Agreed at r6–7: agent-2 iron/copper; agent-1 research, coal, stone. Only research was kept.
- **Agent-1:** r10 one coal drill, then left coal; r31–100 its own iron outpost north of the crash (6 burner drills, 3 furnaces); r104–146 carried coal to agent-2's base; r147–160 steam power; r274–314 gear → red-science → lab chain; r316–388 eight electric drills on iron/copper; r389–430 green-science chain; r433–500 electric drills and belt on coal.
- **Agent-2:** r7–48 first iron drill, chopping wood for fuel; r49–71 took over coal, unasked; r73–123 iron and copper columns, crafted the lab; r161–191 placed and powered the lab; from r192 a courier loop (coal patch → refuel base → plates to lab), adding copper columns, coal chests and stone drills.

The working split emerged in chat at r137–146 and held for 350 rounds: agent-1 power, science, electrification; agent-2 raw materials and haulage.

## 3. Coordination costs
- **Duplication:** agent-1's outpost duplicated agent-2's job, was never revisited after r104 and is absent from its r244 summary. Both announced "Electronics done. Next trigger = craft a Lab" (r103/104).
- **Hand-offs:** r166–176 agent-1 tried dropping furnaces on the ground before building a chest. r243–273 it waited at the lab, followed agent-2's minimap label into a forest, then hunted for the chest; the lab starved (19 packs in rounds 200–300).
- **Undoing:** agent-1 mined the delivery chest (r396); agent-2 could not find it (r407–410) and placed a new one where agent-1 wanted an inserter (r416).
- **Stale world:** vanished fuel alerts were read as "maybe agent-2 refuelled" (r136, r143, r263, r268). At r111 agent-1 came to fuel, assumed it was done, left, and was recalled at r124.
- **Rounds lost (est. from reasoning text):** agent-1 about 57 (11%), agent-2 about 31 (6%), plus 23 of agent-2's rounds on agent-1's coal job. Chat itself was cheap: no chat-only turns.
- **Idle tails (est. from action durations):** each agent's own batch averaged 4.3 s and 5.0 s (solo 4.3 s) but rounds lasted 6.8 s; agent-1 spent about 38% of game time waiting for the round to end, agent-2 28%.

## 4. Gains
- Parallel sites: r147–160 agent-1 built the power plant while agent-2 grew the coal chest to four drills and crafted 7 packs; r433–463 pole line and coal drills while agent-2 added a copper column and stone mining.
- Hauler plus builder: agent-2's deliveries (500 iron + 300 copper at r378) let agent-1 build the green chain without leaving the lab.
- Error catching: agent-2 powered agent-1's dead copper drill (r394–401) and caught the boiler at 7 coal (r465).

## 6. Better or bigger?
Bigger. Team: 40 burner + 11 electric drills, 21 furnaces, 6 assemblers, 1 lab, 1 steam engine. Solo: 19 + 17 drills, 14 furnaces, 2 assemblers, 1 lab, 2 engines (inferred from its 1.8 MW). Agent-2's r476 screenshot shows rows of drill → furnace columns, all fuelled and emptied by hand. The team's extra automation was a green-science chain that made zero packs, a boiler buffer chest, and a 133-belt coal line that never reached the boiler. It was less electrified (22% of drills against 47%).

## 7. For the benchmark
- Never report round-for-round alone; compare and budget at fixed game time and fixed spend.
- Max-of-batches wastes about a third of agent-time. Cap batch length, or re-query each agent when its own batch ends; the prompt never states the rule.
- Simultaneous replies make messages cross. Give one seat the tie-break; deliver unread chat as text, not faded pixels.
- Agents had no coordinates and no hand-off; decide whether map tags and chest drops are skills to measure or hints to give.
- Log per-agent production and idle time; the labour split here came from reasoning text.
- One run per arm on one seed: add repeats and a 1,000-turn solo before trusting any ratio.
