# Opus 5.5 solo, seed 1001: run review

The run reached rung 10 of 24 (assembler placed) and 5 technologies in 500 turns. It ran out of turns, not game time, with a factory still fed by hand across four distant sites. Two harness artifacts made clicking and placing harder than in a normal game (section 7).

**Method.** Scripts in `/tmp/review` parse `log.jsonl`; I read all 500 turns and viewed 28 screenshots. Anything not marked "inferred" was checked against the log or a screenshot.

## 3. Failure modes

- **Wrong recipe after grid positions shifted:** four boilers instead of drills (T88, two placed on coal at T99); pipe-to-ground instead of the pump (T153–158).
- **Shift-click crafts everything:** 12 furnaces (T31), 57 science packs (T215), 76 (T263).
- **Blind placement:** drills T60–71; steam engine T173–189 (17 turns).
- **Wrong machine opened or fuelled:** T258–262, T306–311, T332–333.
- **Extra Escape opened the game menu:** T85, T161, T206, T216, T265.
- **Typing with the search box unfocused fired hotkeys:** T416–421.
- **Stuck on wreckage, trees or its own drills:** T438–440, T451–453, T479.
- **Tree chopping unreliable:** T17–20, T357–362.
- **Unsure where things are:** T192–195, T441–442.

## 4. Long-horizon coherence

Plans persisted. All six summaries carry the same chain (burner mining, steam, lab, automation, electric drills, green science, oil, silo), and each one's next steps were done in the following segment.

By design each summary describes the state six turns before the compaction (the last six turns are kept verbatim). So "current position" was stale every time ("standing at coal field" at T168, when it was at the lake), but it never redid a finished step.

Wrong beliefs it acted on:
- "Shift-click = craft 5" sat in summaries 1–3. Summary 3 recorded the 57-pack accident but kept the rule, and T263 repeated it. Corrected at T264.
- Summary 4 dropped that `hold_key` takes fractions. At T345 it believed durations must be integers and laid poles in 1 s steps until T356.

## 5. Competence

- Batching: 7.4 actions per turn, up to 43 (T334 refuelled four drills).
- Learned shortcuts: crafting search (T159), queue cancel (T243), right-click crafts 5 (T281), ctrl-click transfer (T396), diagonal walking (T391).
- Sound designs: self-feeding coal drills; drills inserting straight into furnaces; paired electric drills into a shared chest (verified, T390); pipes to clear the shoreline (T184–189); chest and inserter buffering the boiler (T463–466).

## 6. What limited progress; final factory

- **Turns:** median 2.85 game seconds per turn.
- **No logistics:** one belt placed (by mistake) and one inserter. Plates, coal and science were carried by hand between base, stone, coal (~70 tiles south) and power (~100 tiles east); six coal trips.
- **Fuel starvation:** copper output was flat from T375 to T425; research sat at 26% from about T419 to T475.

Final state: 17 electric drills (4 coal, 9 iron, 4 copper), 13 furnaces, 2 assemblers, 1 lab, 1 boiler with 2 engines (1.8 MW), 87 science packs made, logistic science pack at 53%, 44 belts crafted but none laid.

## 7. Harness

- **Action errors: none.** 0 of 3,705 actions have `error` or `skipped`; `notes` is always empty.
- **Clicks act on the stale selection (verified T304–305).** A click 50 ms after the pointer jump opened the previously hovered furnace. The model's workaround (move, wait 0.4 s, click) was used 185 times, about 123 s of game time. Inferred: this also explains first-click placement failures (T10, T61).
- **Build preview missing when paused.** Six end-of-turn shots with a building in hand show only an icon (T60, T169, T171, T378, T459, T484); a mid-turn zoom at T460 shows the ghost. Mechanism unknown.
- **Timing clean:** UPS 56.2–60.0; compactions cost 19–30 s wall, no game time. Overhead is about 0.5 game seconds per turn plus 0.13 s per action.
- **Scoring:** `built:` counts placements. `built:boiler` (T99) and `built:transport-belt` (T497) were mistakes; rung 6 fired on a test placement (T183), six turns before the engine was connected.
- **Thinking field:** T398 contains "Sorry, I can't help with that."
- No stale end-of-turn screenshot found in my sample.
